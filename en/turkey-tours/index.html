Quick guide

How to plan your trip to Turkey?

Istanbul is the natural starting point for most trips: historic sights, shopping, Bosphorus cruises and restaurants for every taste. From there, Sapanca and Bursa are easy to reach by road in a day or two.

If you are looking for nature and cool summer weather, head to Turkey's Black Sea region: Trabzon, Uzungöl and the Rize plateaus — and the domestic flight from Istanbul takes no more than about two hours.

For sea and resort lovers there are Antalya and Bodrum, while the famous balloon experience is found in Cappadocia.

Only one week? Start with the Turkey 7-day program with one hotel in Istanbul. Before booking, check the entry requirements for your nationality in the Turkey visa guide for Arab travelers.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about tourism in Turkey

What are the best tourist cities in Turkey for families?

Istanbul for shopping and sights, Trabzon and Uzungöl for nature and cool weather, Sapanca and Bursa for lakes and mountains near Istanbul, and Antalya for resorts and beaches. Many families combine Istanbul with a nature destination in one trip.

How many days are enough to visit Turkey?

5 days are enough for Istanbul alone, 7–8 days for Istanbul with Bursa or Sapanca, and 10 days or more to combine Istanbul with Turkey's Black Sea region (Trabzon and Uzungöl). We design the program around the days you have.

What is the best time to travel to Turkey?

Spring (April–June) and autumn (September–October) are ideal for mild weather; summer is the season for families and Turkey's Black Sea region, while winter suits snow lovers at Uludağ, and hotel prices are generally lower then.

What is Turkey's Black Sea region and what are its main cities?

It refers to the Black Sea region in northeastern Turkey, and its main destinations are Trabzon, Uzungöl, Rize, the Ayder Plateau, Sümela Monastery and more. It is known for forests, mist, green plateaus and refreshing summer weather.

Is a private driver or a group tour better?

A private driver gives families freedom, privacy and flexibility with timing and stops, and many families prefer it, while group tours are more economical for some day trips. We offer both.

How much does a week-long trip to Turkey cost?

The cost varies by season, hotel category, number of travelers and type of car, so we send you a detailed quote based on your trip details instead of a general figure that may not suit you.
