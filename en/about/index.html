About Us

OYA Tourism… because every journey is a story

A travel agency from Istanbul. We craft carefully arranged trips for travelers: from the ticket and the visa to the hotel, the driver and the program — with the attention you deserve.

Our story

We stitch your trip together the way oya lace is made

In Turkish tradition, “Oya” is a fine handmade needlework in which threads are knotted one by one until they become flowers and patterns that adorn the edges of a scarf. We love this meaning because it is exactly what we do: we gather the threads of your trip — flights, hotel, visa, driver and program — and stitch them together with care until they become a complete story.

We started out from Istanbul, the city that connects East and West, to be the bridge between travelers and the most beautiful destinations in Turkey and the world. We know what travelers look for: privacy for the family, halal food, an English-speaking driver, and flexibility in the program.

Because we believe trust is built on clarity, we send every customer a written quote with details, prices and the cancellation policy before any payment, and we stay with them on WhatsApp from the first message until they are back home.
