Spanish Super Cup 2027 in Istanbul: The Complete Fan Guide — From Ticket to Bosphorus Evening

A complete guide to attending the Spanish Super Cup 2027 in Istanbul: dates, the three stadiums and how to reach them, the official ticket, where to stay, your match-day plan, Bosphorus evenings, trips and places we recommend, and how we stay with you from your first message until you are back home.

For the first time, fans of Spanish football are turning their eyes to Turkey, as Istanbul hosts the Spanish Super Cup 2027 as announced, with Barcelona, Atlético Madrid, Real Madrid and Real Sociedad taking part. The final could be a clásico between Barcelona and Real Madrid if both teams win their semi-finals.

But a tournament trip is bigger than ninety minutes. It involves flights, a hotel and getting around a big city, matches that kick off at ten at night in a cold winter, and free days between matches that deserve to be put to good use. In this guide we have gathered everything you need, step by step: the dates, the stadiums and how to reach them, the official ticket, accommodation, your match-day plan, Bosphorus evenings, trips and places we recommend, and how we stay with you from your first message until you return home.

We would point out from the start that tournament details come from the organizing body, and dates, stadiums or kick-off times may change. So we keep checking for updates and confirm them with you when you book.

The Tournament at a Glance

  • City: Istanbul, Turkey.
  • Teams: Barcelona, Atlético Madrid, Real Madrid, Real Sociedad.
  • Format: two semi-finals and then a final, which means three matches in five days.
  • Stadiums: Şükrü Saracoğlu in Kadıköy, Tüpraş in Beşiktaş, and RAMS Park in Sarıyer.
  • Kick-off time: 22:00 Istanbul time (20:00 Spain time).
  • After the tournament: the blessed month of Ramadan begins around February 8, 2027.

These dates are as announced up to the date this article was updated:

Matches end around midnight, so we recommend booking an extra night after each match you want to attend, especially the night of the final, so you are not tied to a flight at tight timing.

Saudi Arabia has hosted many editions of the Spanish Super Cup in recent years, but the 2027 edition coincides with the Kingdom hosting the AFC Asian Cup 2027, which is why the tournament has moved to Istanbul this time, as announced.

The Three Stadiums and How to Reach Them

Each match has its own stadium, and the three stadiums are in different parts of the city. That is why we choose your hotel according to the match you will attend, and arrange private round-trip transport for you.

Şükrü Saracoğlu Stadium — Kadıköy (first semi-final)

Fenerbahçe's stadium on the Asian side of the city, with a capacity of about 47,000 spectators. It is near Söğütlüçeşme station, where the Marmaray train and the Metrobus meet, and within walking distance of Kadıköy Market and the ferry pier. The area is lively and full of restaurants and cafés, and it is an excellent place for an early dinner before the match.

Tüpraş Stadium — Beşiktaş (second semi-final)

Beşiktaş's stadium on the European bank of the Bosphorus next to Dolmabahçe Palace, with a capacity of about 42,000 spectators. You can reach it on foot from Taksim Square in about fifteen minutes, or from Kabataş station, where the tram ends and the funicular from Taksim arrives. Its central location makes it the easiest of the three stadiums.

RAMS Park — Sarıyer (the final)

Galatasaray's stadium in the Sarıyer district in the north of the city, and the largest of the three stadiums with about 53,000 seats. It is about ten kilometers from Taksim and is served by the M2 metro line, but the surrounding roads get very congested before and after the match. That is why, on the night of the final in particular, we recommend private transport with an early departure time and a clear meeting point after the whistle.

You will find the dates, the stadiums and the 3D stand model on the Spanish Super Cup 2027 tickets page, and we update it whenever there is news about the tournament.

The Official Ticket: How to Get It Safely

  • Buy only from the official channel: the channels approved by the organizer, either directly or with the help of a travel agency that deals transparently. Avoid anonymous sellers on social media.
  • The ticket in your name: tickets for these tournaments are usually issued in the name of the person, and your passport or ID may be requested at entry.
  • Link your bookings to the ticket: do not pay for flights and the hotel until the ticket picture is clear, or choose bookings with flexible cancellation.
  • Mind the seat location: each team's fan stands are usually separate, so choose the seat that suits your loyalties.

OYA Tourism is an independent travel agency. We are not an official body affiliated with the Spanish federation or any club, and we do not sell tickets. We help you buy your ticket from the official channel, in your own name and at its official price, with a declared service fee, and we explain the terms in writing before any payment. If you prefer to buy it yourself, we guide you step by step and arrange the rest of the trip for you.

Where to stay in Istanbul during the tournament

The golden rule: stay near your match's stadium or near a main transport line, not just near a tourist landmark. Congestion on match-day evenings can make a car ride long, while the metro stays faster and more reliable.

We expect hotel demand to rise during tournament week as fans arrive from Spain and abroad, so it is advisable to book early with a flexible cancellation policy. We recommend the option that suits your group size and budget through our hotel booking service.

Arrival in Istanbul

  • Choose the right airport: Istanbul Airport is on the European side, and Sabiha Gökçen Airport is on the Asian side, and the distance between them is large. If you will attend only the first semi-final, Sabiha Gökçen may be closer to your hotel.
  • Book flights early: prices usually rise as the date approaches, and direct flights are available from many cities. We compare the options for you on the flight booking page.
  • Airport pickup: our driver waits for you in the arrivals hall and takes you to the hotel in a private car, with no hunting for a taxi or haggling after a long flight. The service details are on the airport pickup page.
  • Stay connected: our fan packages include a Turkish SIM card, so your phone stays connected for maps, your ticket and contacting us from the moment you arrive.
  • Entry visa: it varies by nationality; some Arab nationalities enter without a visa, and others need an e-Visa or a visa from the embassy. Check the Turkey visa guide before booking flights.

Your Match-Day Plan, Step by Step

  1. A calm day: keep match-day outings light and close to your hotel, because the match ends late.
  2. Late lunch or early dinner: eat before you set out, because the lines around the stadiums are long and the food options inside are limited.
  3. Set out early: allow two to three hours before kick-off to arrive and get through the security checks.
  4. Carry only what you need: your passport or ID, and your ticket on your phone with a charged battery, and avoid large bags because they may be refused.
  5. Dress for the cold: the open stands are cold in February, so bring a coat, gloves and a scarf.
  6. Agree on a meeting point: decide with your group and the driver on a clear place to meet after the match, because the phone network sometimes weakens in the crowd.
  7. Head back calmly: leaving the stadium is slow, and your driver stays in touch with you until he takes you back to your hotel.

More Than a Match: What We Can Arrange for You

Between the semi-finals and the final there are two full days, a chance to experience Istanbul properly. These are the main experiences we arrange for tournament guests, and we choose with you what suits your group and budget:

A Bosphorus evening

The most beautiful way to see Istanbul is from the water. You can choose an evening seafood dinner with a view of the lit palaces and bridges, or a private yacht for your family or group, subject to availability, or a daytime boat tour between Eminönü and Rumeli Fortress. In the offer we explain the type of evening, the food and the duration before you book, and for families we choose suitable options.

City tours with an English-speaking driver

The old city: Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, Topkapı Palace, the Basilica Cistern and the Grand Bazaar, with a licensed guide on request. And a drive along the Bosphorus between Ortaköy, Bebek and Çamlıca Hill on the Asian side. Take a look at our private driver service and day tours.

A snow trip to Uludağ

Early February is usually snow season on Mount Uludağ. A day trip to Bursa and Uludağ combines the cable car, playing in the snow and Bursa's famous dishes, and returns to Istanbul in the evening. If you want more quiet, Sapanca and Maşukiye in their winter scenery are a nearby option. You can also combine the matches with the Istanbul and Uludağ Winter 6 Days program.

Princes' Islands and quiet days

Büyükada, with its wooden houses and car-free streets, suits a quiet day after the match night. The driver takes you to the ferry pier and waits for you on your return.

Shopping

The Grand Bazaar and the Spice Bazaar for gifts and sweets (the Grand Bazaar is closed on Sunday), and modern malls such as İstinye Park, Zorlu Center and Cevahir. We arrange a shopping day with a private car that carries you and your purchases between places with no hassle.

For football lovers

Istanbul's big clubs, Fenerbahçe, Beşiktaş and Galatasaray, have museums telling their history, and visits can be arranged according to their opening hours, which may change on match days. For photography fans, the three stadiums look completely different on match nights.

Places We Recommend

  • For views: Pierre Loti Hill in Eyüp with the cable car above the Golden Horn, Çamlıca Hill on the Asian side, and Galata Tower.
  • For an evening walk: İstiklal Avenue with its historic tram, the waterfront in Kadıköy and Moda, and Ortaköy square under the Bosphorus Bridge.
  • For street food: a fish sandwich (balık ekmek) at the Eminönü pier, simit with tea, and kumpir and waffles in Ortaköy.
  • For a Turkish breakfast: the breakfast restaurant street in Beşiktaş, a perfect spot for the morning after the match.
  • For sweets: Turkish baklava and kunafa in long-established shops such as Karaköy Güllüoğlu and Hafız Mustafa.
  • For winter drinks: hot salep, and boza at the historic Vefa Bozacısı, among the pleasures of Istanbul in the cold.
  • For prayer: mosques in every neighborhood, and Friday, February 5 is a chance to perform Friday prayer in one of the historic mosques such as Süleymaniye Mosque or the Blue Mosque.

Halal food is available in most Istanbul restaurants. If you wish, we suggest restaurants to suit your taste and budget and book the table for you, especially before the match when restaurants near the stadiums get crowded.

One match in 4 days

The first day is for arrival and a Bosphorus evening, the second for a city tour and then the match in the evening, the third for shopping or the snow trip, and the fourth for the return. It is the most suitable option for those following a single team or traveling on a short vacation.

For families with children

One match is often enough, because evening matches in the cold are tiring for little ones. Devote the remaining days to the snow on Uludağ, the Princes' Islands and Miniatürk park, with a hotel close to public transport and a private car throughout the stay.

Practical Information

  • Weather: February is one of Istanbul's coldest months, and temperatures are usually between a few degrees above zero and about ten degrees, with frequent rain and a chance of snow. Bring a small umbrella and comfortable shoes that the rain will not ruin.
  • Public transport: the Istanbulkart works on the metro, tram, Metrobus, ferries, buses and the Marmaray train.
  • Currency and payment: the Turkish lira is the local currency, cards are accepted in most restaurants and shops, and exchange rates at exchange offices in the city are often better than at the airport.
  • Insurance: we recommend travel insurance that covers medical treatment for the whole length of the trip.
  • Ramadan: Ramadan 1448 begins around February 8, 2027, so if you prefer to return before it starts, book your return flight early because demand rises in those days.
  • Stadium atmosphere: cheer on your team with enthusiasm, avoid friction with the other team's fans, and follow the security instructions.

Pre-Travel Checklist

  • A valid passport, and an entry visa if your nationality requires one.
  • Confirmation of your official ticket in your name, and knowing the stadium entry conditions.
  • Flight and hotel booking, with an extra night after the match.
  • Confirmation of airport pickup and transport to the stadium and back.
  • Travel insurance, and warm winter clothes.
  • OYA's WhatsApp number saved on your phone.

Related questions

When are the Spanish Super Cup 2027 matches held in Istanbul?

As announced: the first semi-final (Barcelona v Atlético Madrid) on Tuesday, February 2, 2027, the second semi-final (Real Sociedad v Real Madrid) on Wednesday, February 3, and the final on Saturday, February 6. All matches are at 22:00 Istanbul time.

Why was the tournament not held in Saudi Arabia this time?

Because its date coincides with Saudi Arabia hosting the 2027 Asian Cup, so the 2027 edition was moved to Istanbul, as announced.

Is OYA an official seller of Spanish Super Cup tickets?

No, we are not an official body affiliated with the Spanish federation or the clubs. We do not sell tickets: we help you buy them from the official channel and in your own name, and we arrange your hotel and transfers.

What is the best area to stay in for the matches?

It depends on the match: Kadıköy or Üsküdar for the first semi-final, Beşiktaş or Taksim for the second semi-final, and Şişli or Maslak for the final. For those attending the whole tournament, we choose a central hotel close to public transport.

Do you stay in touch with us during the trip?

Yes. Our English-speaking team follows up with you on WhatsApp every day from 9 AM to 11 PM, and on match nights your driver stays in touch with you until he takes you back to your hotel.

Can you arrange a Bosphorus evening for the family?

Yes, we can arrange an evening seafood dinner or a private yacht for your family or group, subject to availability, and in the offer we explain the type of evening, the food and the duration before you book.

Can I combine the match with a trip to the snow?

Yes. Early February is usually snow season on Mount Uludağ, and you can add a day trip to Bursa and Uludağ or to Sapanca between the semi-finals and the final.

Do I need a visa to enter Turkey for the tournament?

It depends on your nationality: some Arab nationalities enter without a visa, and others need an e-Visa or a visa from the embassy. See the Turkey visa guide on our website or ask us on WhatsApp.
