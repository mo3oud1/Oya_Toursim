Overview

The family visit visa starts with the host living in Saudi Arabia, who submits the visit request on the visa platform of the Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs and receives a request number to send to you. The platform specifies which degrees of kinship can be invited, first-degree relatives above all.

Once the request number is issued, the visitor completes the procedures from their country of residence. For residents of Turkey, the application is made to the Saudi Embassy in Ankara or the Consulate General in Istanbul through the accredited visa center, with a prior appointment and in-person attendance.

The visa may be issued for a single entry or multiple entries, and the length of stay is set in the visa itself. Please note that in February 2025 the Saudi authorities announced that citizens of 14 countries, including Egypt, Iraq, Jordan, Yemen, the Maghreb countries and Sudan, would be limited to a single-entry visa of no more than 30 days instead of the one-year multiple-entry visa. We have not found an official announcement cancelling this decision, and we check the updated status for your nationality before applying.

At OYA Tourism, our role is to review the request number and the host's details, prepare, translate and certify the kinship documents when needed, book the appointment and insurance, and then book your flights after approval. We are a service office, not a government body, and the decision rests with the Saudi authorities alone.

Who can apply?

  • Relatives of people legally resident in the Kingdom, within the degrees of kinship the platform allows, such as parents, spouses and children.
  • Turkish citizens living in Turkey.
  • Arab nationals living in Turkey with a valid residence permit, subject to checking the conditions for their nationality.

Types and options

Types of family visit visa

Single-entry family visit

Suited to a visit on set dates, with the length of stay specified in the issued visa. It is the option currently available to citizens of the countries covered by the February 2025 restrictions.

Multiple-entry family visit

Allows more than one entry during its validity. It is granted at the discretion of the competent authorities and depending on the visitor's nationality, and may not be available to some nationalities.

Checklist

Required papers and documents

The general list is below, and requirements may vary by nationality and country of residence — we confirm them for you before you apply.

  • A passport valid for at least 6 months, and a recent passport-style photo with a white background.
  • The visit request number issued by the visa platform, which the host sends to you.
  • A copy of the host's residence permit in Saudi Arabia.
  • Proof of kinship: a birth certificate, marriage contract or family register, translated and certified as required.
  • Valid medical insurance covering the length of the visit.
  • For non-Turkish applicants: a copy of the valid Turkish residence permit (both sides).
We cannot start your file without the request number issued by the host inside the Kingdom.
Attending the visa center in person at the set appointment is required, so bring the originals with the copies.
Kinship documents issued outside Turkey may need additional translation and certification, so start preparing them early.
We do not publish unconfirmed fees. We confirm the updated government fees when you apply, and they are usually not refundable if the application is rejected.
The family visit visa does not permit work or Hajj. The final decision rests with the Saudi authorities, and approval cannot be guaranteed.

Questions & answers

Family visit visa FAQ

What are the requirements for a Saudi family visit visa?

You need a relative living in the Kingdom with legal residency, within the degrees of kinship the platform allows. He or she submits the visit request and sends you its number. You then need a passport valid for 6 months, proof of kinship that is translated and certified, and medical insurance, and then you apply at the visa center.

How do I get a Saudi family visit visa from Turkey?

Your relative submits the request from inside Saudi Arabia and sends you the request number. We then prepare your file and book you an appointment at the visa center accredited by the embassy in Ankara or the consulate in Istanbul. You attend in person to hand in the file, and we follow up with you until the decision is issued.

Can a non-Turkish resident of Turkey apply for the family visit from here?

Yes, a resident of Turkey with a valid residence permit can apply through the visa center in Turkey, attaching a copy of the residence permit. However, some nationalities are subject to restrictions on the type and duration of the visa, so we check your case before starting.

How much is the Saudi family visit visa fee?

Websites circulate different fee figures, and we have not found an updated official figure to rely on, so we do not publish an amount that may be inaccurate. We confirm the government fees, insurance and our service fee clearly when the application is opened.

How long can I stay on a family visit visa?

The length of stay and type of entry are set in the issued visa, and they vary by application and nationality. Citizens of the countries covered by the February 2025 restrictions currently receive a single-entry visa of no more than 30 days, unless announced otherwise.

Does the family visit visa allow performing Hajj?

No. No visit visa, including the family visit visa, permits Hajj, and the Saudi authorities have announced penalties for violators. Hajj has its own visa and permit, available only through official channels.
