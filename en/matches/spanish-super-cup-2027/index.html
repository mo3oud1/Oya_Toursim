About the tournament

Why is the 2027 edition different?

The four-team Spanish Super Cup has usually been held in Saudi Arabia in recent years, but the 2027 edition moves to Istanbul because its dates coincide with Saudi Arabia hosting the 2027 Asian Cup, as announced by the Royal Spanish Football Federation.

The tournament brings together the league champion and runner-up and the cup winner and runner-up: Barcelona, Real Madrid, Real Sociedad and Atlético Madrid. If Barcelona and Real Madrid both qualify, the final will be a Clásico in Istanbul.

Because OYA Tourism is based in Istanbul, we arrange your whole trip on the ground: the hotel, the pickup, transfers to the stadium, and even a sightseeing program before or after the match in Istanbul, Bursa and Sapanca.

Tips for attending a match in Istanbul

Book early

Barcelona and Real Madrid matches are in huge demand; booking early means better categories and seats.

Names as in your passport

Tickets may be issued in the holders' names, so send names that match your passports.

Plan your transport

Match-day traffic in Istanbul is heavy; private transfers arranged in advance save you a lot.

February is cold

The weather in Istanbul is cold and sometimes rainy in February — bring a warm coat for the stands.

Make it a trip

Add a tour of Istanbul or a snow trip to Uludağ in Bursa before or after the match.

Beware of the black market

Do not buy tickets from unknown sources on social media; always ask for written confirmation.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about the Spanish Super Cup 2027

Where and when is the Spanish Super Cup 2027 held?

As announced by the Royal Spanish Football Federation: the first semi-final (Barcelona × Atlético Madrid) on Tuesday, February 2, 2027 at Şükrü Saracoğlu Stadium (Chobani) in Kadıköy, the second semi-final (Real Sociedad × Real Madrid) on Wednesday, February 3 at Tüpraş Stadium in Beşiktaş, and the final on Saturday, February 6 at RAMS Park. All matches kick off at 22:00 Istanbul time.

Why is the tournament held in Istanbul and not in Saudi Arabia?

As announced, the tournament's usual dates coincide with Saudi Arabia hosting the 2027 Asian Cup, so the 2027 edition moved to Istanbul, with a return to Saudi Arabia announced for later.

Which teams take part?

Barcelona, Real Madrid, Atlético Madrid and Real Sociedad. The draw paired Barcelona and Atlético Madrid in the first semi-final, and Real Sociedad and Real Madrid in the second semi-final.

When should I submit my request?

The earlier the better; Barcelona and Real Madrid matches are in high demand. Register your interest now on WhatsApp and we will let you know the prices and availability as soon as official sales open.

What if the official ticket is not available?

Tickets are sold through the official channel and subject to availability, and nobody other than the organizer can guarantee them. So, before any payment, we explain to you in writing what is refundable and what is not refundable in the package if the ticket cannot be obtained or the match date changes.

Where should I stay in Istanbul during the tournament?

It depends on your match: for the first semi-final (Kadıköy), hotels on the Asian side in Kadıköy and Üsküdar suit you; for the second semi-final (Beşiktaş), hotels in Beşiktaş, Taksim and Şişli; and for the final (RAMS Park in Sarıyer), hotels in Şişli, Levent and Maslak. Because matches end around midnight, we recommend a private transfer arranged in advance.

Do I need a visa to enter Turkey to attend the match?

It depends on your nationality; citizens of many Arab countries, including the Gulf countries, enter Turkey without a visa for a set period, while other nationalities need an e-Visa or an embassy visa. Send us your nationality and we will tell you what is required.
