Bosnia 7-Day Tour: Sarajevo, Mostar and Kravica Falls

Sarajevo with its Ottoman spirit, Mostar and its famous bridge, and the nature of the Neretva River, Lake Jablanica and the Kravica Waterfalls in one week.

Sarajevo → Konjic → Jablanica → MostarFamilyNatureCities

About the program

Tourism in Bosnia is a different experience in the heart of the Balkans: Ottoman mosques, old bazaars, emerald-colored rivers and forests blanketing the mountains. The Bosnia 7-day tour takes you from Sarajevo to Mostar via Konjic and Jablanica, with a visit to the Buna spring in Blagaj and Kravica Falls. Bosnia carries a living Ottoman heritage, seen in its stone bridges, fountains and minarets, alongside green mountain scenery that makes every road a journey in itself.

The flight from Istanbul to Sarajevo is short, about two hours, and the country feels familiar to visitors, with plenty of halal food and the call to prayer filling the old neighborhoods. We divide the stay between four nights in Sarajevo and two nights in Mostar, with private-car tours that let you stop at every view worth a photo. We set a relaxed pace that suits older travelers and children, with stops for prayer and meals at selected places, and the tour can be extended to visit Bihać and the Una River.

Trip highlights

  • Baščaršija and the Gazi Husrev-beg Mosque in the heart of Sarajevo
  • Trebević cable car and the Yellow Fortress view at sunset
  • Vrelo Bosne park and the Tunnel of Hope Museum
  • Konjic's old bridge and Lake Jablanica
  • The Old Bridge in Mostar and the Koski Mehmed Pasha Mosque
  • Blagaj Tekke at the Buna River spring
  • Kravica Falls and the historic village of Počitelj

The program day by day

  1. Day1

    Arrival in Sarajevo

    We meet you at Sarajevo International Airport and transfer you to the hotel. After some rest, we wander through Baščaršija, the old Ottoman bazaar, with its famous wooden fountain and cafés serving Bosnian coffee. A first evening in an atmosphere that recalls the old lanes of Istanbul.

    • Sarajevo International Airport
    • Baščaršija
    • Sebilj fountain
  2. Day2

    Sarajevo Tour

    We visit the Gazi Husrev-beg Mosque, Bosnia's most famous mosque, then pass the historic City Hall (Vijećnica) and the Latin Bridge over the Miljacka River. In the afternoon we ride the Trebević cable car for wide views over the city, and finish at the Yellow Fortress at sunset.

    • Gazi Husrev-beg Mosque
    • City Hall (Vijećnica)
    • Latin Bridge
    • Trebević cable car
    • Yellow Fortress
  3. Day3

    Source of the Bosna and the Tunnel of Hope

    We head to the suburb of Ilidža to visit the Vrelo Bosne park, with its clear streams, swans and horse-drawn carriages. Then we visit the Tunnel of Hope Museum, which tells the story of the city's resilience in the 1990s. The day can be completed with a trip to Mount Igman or free time for shopping.

    • Ilidža
    • Vrelo Bosne park
    • Tunnel of Hope Museum
    • Mount Igman
  4. Day4

    To Mostar via Konjic and Jablanica

    We leave Sarajevo to the south along the Neretva River road and stop in Konjic at its old stone bridge. Then we reach Jablanica and its turquoise lake, visit the historic bridge site on the Neretva, and taste the roast lamb the town is famous for. We arrive in Mostar in the evening to see the Old Bridge lit up.

    • Konjic Old Bridge
    • Lake Jablanica
    • Battle of Neretva Museum
    • Mostar
  5. Day5

    Mostar, Blagaj and Kravica Falls

    In the morning we walk through the Old Town and the Kujundžiluk bazaar, and climb the minaret of the Koski Mehmed Pasha Mosque for the best view of the Old Bridge. Then we visit the Blagaj Tekke at the spring of the Buna River, which emerges from inside the mountain, and the stone village of Počitelj, and we finish at Kravica Falls, where you can swim in summer.

    • The Old Bridge (Stari Most)
    • Koski Mehmed Pasha Mosque
    • Blagaj Tekke
    • Počitelj
    • Kravica Falls
  6. Day6

    Back to Sarajevo

    After breakfast we return to Sarajevo on a scenic mountain road alongside the river. In the afternoon there is free time to shop in Sarajevo's malls or buy copper crafts at the Coppersmiths' Bazaar in Baščaršija, followed by a farewell evening in the Old Town.

    • Neretva Valley
    • Shopping malls in Sarajevo
    • Coppersmiths' Bazaar
  7. Day7

    Departure

    Breakfast at the hotel and free time according to your flight, then a transfer to Sarajevo International Airport for your flight back to Istanbul.

    • Hotel
    • Sarajevo International Airport

Questions & answers

Questions about the Bosnia 7-Day Tour

Do travelers need a visa for Bosnia?

It depends on nationality: some nationalities enter without a visa, some benefit from seasonal exemptions or from valid visas for other countries, and others need a visa from the embassy. Send us your nationality and we will confirm the current requirements. The final decision rests with the official authorities in Bosnia.

How many days are enough to visit Bosnia?

7 days is an ideal length for Sarajevo, Mostar and the nature around them. If you want more, you can add Bihać or extend your stay in Sarajevo.

When are Kravica Falls at their most beautiful?

In spring the water is abundant and the view is powerful, and in summer the water is suitable for swimming. Swimming may be restricted at certain times depending on water levels and the site management's decisions.

Does the program include flights?

It is not included by default, and can be added on request through our flight ticket service.
