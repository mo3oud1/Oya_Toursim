Uzungöl hotels and cabins: a guide to staying by the lake

Uzungöl is a small mountain village around a calm lake, and staying a night or two gives you its best hours: early morning and the evening after the day visitors leave.

Uzungöl hotels: what you should know

Uzungöl hotels (also written “Uzungol”) range from small and mid-sized hotels around the lake to wooden cabins and bungalows on the hillsides and countryside houses in the surrounding villages. The village is small and sits a little over a thousand meters above sea level, so do not expect huge resorts, but warm, mountain-style accommodation where the view is the real star, and where nights are cool even in summer. The village is about two hours by car from Trabzon Airport, along a mountain road that passes green valleys and small villages beside a mountain river, so many people prefer to stay overnight rather than return the same day.

Hotels near the lake are comfortable for older adults and children, because the walkway, restaurants and mosque are steps away, but summer nights there are busier. Cabins on the hillsides give you privacy and a panoramic view, and some have a jacuzzi or fireplace, though some are on very steep uphill roads that need a car. If your family is large, the countryside houses in the neighboring villages are roomier and quieter. The village also has simple lodges that are enough for one night for those who spend the day outdoors and want to wake up early at the lake before the day-trippers arrive.

Places with a good view are limited and fill up first in July and August, so booking early is worthwhile. Some visitors prefer to stay in Trabzon and visit Uzungöl for a day, while others add one or two nights there as part of a Trabzon and Uzungöl itinerary. In winter the village is covered in snow and fewer options are available, so ask about heating and road conditions before booking. Types of stay

Where to stay in Uzungöl

Lake-view hotels

Small and mid-sized hotels near the walkway, with rooms overlooking the lake or the mountain. Best suited to families with young children and older adults.

Wooden cabins and bungalows

On the hillsides with panoramic views, some with an indoor jacuzzi or fireplace. A quiet, romantic experience, but the spaces are smaller and the roads to them are uphill.

Countryside houses and villas

More space and private gardens in the surrounding villages, suited to large families who want quiet and privacy and have a car to get around.

Simple village lodges

Clean, simple rooms for one night, enough for those who spend most of their time outdoors and want to wake up early at the lake.

Before you book

Check these details

  • Is the lake view from the room itself or only from a common area?
  • Heating in the room, as nights are cool even in summer
  • The nature of the road to the cabin: can any car reach it, and how far is it from the lake on foot?
  • The jacuzzi or fireplace: inside the cabin or shared, and is there an extra charge?
  • Is breakfast included? The Turkish countryside breakfast is one of the best experiences of the place
  • Parking, as parking near the lake is limited at peak times
  • The cancellation policy, especially in winter when snow may affect the roads

OYA tips

Booking tips

  • Book early for summer: cabins and hotels with a good view are the first to fill up in July and August.
  • One night is often enough for those who want the morning and evening at the lake, and two nights for those who want quiet and a visit to the nearby plateaus.
  • If you have a stroller or are traveling with older adults, choose accommodation close to the lake and the walkway and avoid cabins on the slopes.
  • Arrange a private car from Trabzon Airport straight to Uzungöl, as the road is mountainous, about two hours, and public transport is limited.
  • In winter, make sure the road to your accommodation is open and equipped for snow, and bring warm clothes and suitable shoes.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about Uzungöl hotels

Is it better to stay overnight in Uzungöl or visit as a day trip from Trabzon?

A day trip is enough to see the lake and the walkway, but at least one night gives you the early morning and the quiet evening after the visitors leave, which are the village's best hours. Off-season, or for those who prefer city services, staying in Trabzon with a day trip is a practical choice.

How many nights are enough in Uzungöl?

One night is enough for most visitors, and two nights for those who want to relax and visit the nearby plateaus without rushing. Longer suits those staying in a cabin or countryside house and looking for complete quiet.

Are there cabins with a jacuzzi in Uzungöl?

Yes, some cabins and bungalows on the hillsides have an indoor jacuzzi or a fireplace, and they are in demand for honeymoons. Options are limited and sell out early in summer, so ask us well in advance and make sure the jacuzzi is inside the cabin and not shared.

Are Uzungöl hotels suitable for older adults?

Yes, if you choose a hotel close to the lake and the walkway, as restaurants and the mosque are steps away. Cabins on the slopes, however, can be tiring for older adults because of the uphill roads and stairs.

Can I visit Uzungöl and stay overnight in winter?

Yes, the village welcomes visitors in winter and is covered in snow, but accommodation options are fewer and some side roads are affected by snow. We recommend a car with a driver used to mountain roads, and checking that your accommodation has heating.

Is there halal food near Uzungöl hotels?

Yes, the local restaurants around the lake usually serve halal food, the best known being grilled fish, grills and the countryside breakfast. If you are in a remote cabin, there are small grocery stores in the village for basics.
