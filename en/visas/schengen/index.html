Schengen visa from Turkey: documents, appointments and file preparation

A Schengen visa from Turkey lets you visit the European Schengen countries with a single visa, whether you are a Turkish citizen or a resident of Turkey with a valid residence permit. At OYA Tourism we prepare your file in full, help you book your appointment at the visa center, and arrange the insurance and bookings required.

Overview

Schengen applications in Turkey are submitted through external visa centers that differ by country. According to the latest announcements, Germany and Italy use iDATA; France, the Netherlands, Belgium and other countries use VFS Global; Spain uses BLS International; and Greece uses Kosmos for Istanbul residents and VFS in Ankara and Izmir. These providers may change, so we verify them before every booking.

You apply to the country that is your main destination (where you will stay longest), or to the country of first entry if the stays are equal. You can apply up to six months before travel, preferably at least 15 days before, and a decision is usually issued within 15 calendar days, which may extend to 45 days in individual cases. Because appointments in Turkey are under heavy pressure in peak seasons, we advise you to start one to three months before you travel.

Turkish citizens living in Turkey have an important advantage: under rules adopted by the European Union in July 2025, anyone who has obtained two visas and used them lawfully during the previous three years can receive a multiple-entry visa for one year, then for three years, then for five years. It is not automatic, though, and remains subject to the consulate's discretion and the validity of the passport.

Residents of Turkey of other nationalities, such as Syrians, Iraqis and Egyptians, apply at the consulate responsible for their place of legal residence, and the Turkish residence permit (ikamet) is an essential part of the file. Many consulates require the permit to remain valid for a period after the return date, and the period varies from one consulate to another.

Who can apply?

  • Turkish citizens living in Turkey, with the possibility of benefiting from the stepped multiple-entry visa system.
  • Residents of Turkey with a valid residence permit who are nationals of Arab countries (Syrians, Iraqis, Egyptians, Jordanians and others).
  • Employees, business owners, students and retirees, each category with different supporting documents.
  • Families traveling with children, with additional documents for minors.
  • Holders of the temporary protection ID (kimlik): application conditions may differ, so we advise you to consult us before any booking.

Types and options

Types of Schengen visa

Short-stay tourist visa

For tourism and visiting European cities, with a stay of up to 90 days within any 180 days.

Visiting family or friends

Requires an invitation from a host living in a Schengen country, together with their documents.

Business visa

For attending meetings and trade fairs, with an invitation from the host company and a letter from your employer.

Multiple-entry visa (stepped system for Turkish citizens)

One year, then 3 years, then 5 years for those who have used their previous visas lawfully, at the consulate's discretion.

Checklist

Required papers and documents

The general list is below, and requirements may vary by nationality and country of residence — we confirm them for you before you apply.

  • The signed application form and two passport photos that meet biometric specifications.
  • A passport issued within the last 10 years, valid for at least 3 months after the date of departure from the Schengen area, with two blank pages.
  • Copies of previous visas and old passports, if any.
  • Medical travel insurance with coverage of at least 30,000 euros for the entire trip.
  • A round-trip flight reservation and a hotel reservation, or an invitation from the host.
  • A bank statement for the last 3 months.
  • For employees: a letter from your employer stating your leave, your social security record (SGK hizmet dökümü) and payslips.
  • For business owners: tax certificate (Vergi levhası), trade registry gazette and certificate of activity; for students: student certificate (Öğrenci belgesi).
  • Copy of the population register extract (Nüfus kayıt örneği).
  • For foreigners: the Turkish residence permit card (ikamet) with a copy.

Questions & answers

Schengen visa FAQ

Can a resident of Turkey apply for a Schengen visa from Istanbul?

Yes. A resident with a legal residence permit applies at the consulate responsible for their place of residence in Turkey, and includes the residence permit in the file. Many consulates require the permit to remain valid for a period after your return, and we check the requirement of the relevant consulate for you.

What documents are required for a Schengen visa?

In general: passport, photos and application form, travel insurance of at least 30,000 euros, flight and hotel reservations, a bank statement, proof of employment or study, a population register extract, and a residence permit for non-Turkish citizens. Details vary by consulate, and we give you a checklist tailored to your case.

Which Schengen country is the easiest for getting a visa?

No country is "easy" with any guarantee. What matters most is applying to the right country: the country of your main destination, or the country of first entry if the stays are equal in length. Applying to a country you do not really plan to visit can harm your file, while a well-organized, honest file is the most important factor.

How much is the Schengen visa fee?

The official fee is set by the European Union, and the visa center's fee is added to it. Fees may change, so we confirm them with you at the time of application. We organize your file, help you book the appointment and follow up on your application for a small service fee that we explain to you before we start.

How long does it take to get a Schengen visa?

A decision is usually issued within 15 calendar days of submitting the application, and in some cases it can take up to 45 days. In Turkey the biggest challenge is often appointment availability, so start early.

Do you guarantee visa approval or getting an appointment?

No. The decision rests with the consulate alone, and appointments are booked through the visa centers' official websites, subject to availability. What we commit to is a well-organized file, carefully reviewed against the published requirements.
