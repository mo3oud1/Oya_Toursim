Destination guide • Bodrum

Visiting Bodrum: beaches, yachts and resorts

Visiting Bodrum means white houses on green hills, marinas crowded with yachts and quiet bays with clear blue water on the Aegean Sea, with upscale resorts that suit families and couples.

Best time to visit: May to October, with the best swimming from June to SeptemberSuggested duration: 4–7 daysNearest airport: Milas-Bodrum Airport (BJV), about 35 km from central Bodrum

Why Bodrum?

Bodrum at a glance

Bodrum is a peninsula on the Aegean Sea in southwest Turkey. In ancient times it was the city of Halicarnassus, which contained the Mausoleum, one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. Today it is one of the most upscale summer destinations in Turkey, with its marinas, bays and seafood restaurants. It is known for its white cube-shaped houses that recall the nearby Greek islands, and for the olive and citrus trees on its hills. It is a comfortable base for exploring the Aegean coast, from the ancient city of Ephesus in the north to the limestone terraces of Pamukkale.

In the center of town stands Bodrum Castle (the Castle of St. Peter), built by the Knights of St. John in the fifteenth century, which houses a museum of underwater archaeology among the most important of its kind in the world. Around it are the marina, the market and the seafront promenade, while coastal villages with their own character are spread around the peninsula: luxurious Yalıkavak, upscale Göltürkbükü, quiet Bitez, and Gümüşlük with its fish restaurants at sunset. It is a pleasure to stroll in the evening through the old market near the castle to buy local products and gifts.

The best thing about Bodrum is the sea itself: trips on traditional wooden boats (gulets) between the bays and islands to swim in clear water, or a private yacht for your family for a full day. Luxury resorts with private beaches are scattered along the coast, and we choose the ones that are quiet and suitable for families, away from the nightlife areas. Their restaurants are known for fresh fish and seafood, with sunset views in the western bays.

Getting there is easy via Milas-Bodrum Airport (BJV), on domestic flights from Istanbul that take about an hour and a quarter, and in summer seasonal direct flights operate from some cities in the region. We arrange flights, hotel, tours and an English-speaking driver for you, and Bodrum can be combined with Istanbul or with Antalya in a single program. May, June and September suit those who prefer milder weather and fewer crowds, and swimming is especially pleasant in June and September.

Top sights

The best places to visit in Bodrum

A carefully chosen list of what deserves your time in Bodrum, with the visits smartly arranged within our programs.

Bodrum Castle (Castle of St. Peter)

A fifteenth-century stone castle on a spit of land between two bays, with its towers and gardens and a view over the marina.

Museum of Underwater Archaeology

Inside the castle, it displays ancient shipwrecks, jars and treasures recovered from the seabed, and it appeals to children interested in the sea.

Bodrum Marina and the seafront promenade

The yacht harbor in the heart of town, surrounded by restaurants, cafés and shops, at its most beautiful in the evening.

Yalıkavak Marina

The most upscale marina in Bodrum, with luxury yachts, international shops and fine restaurants, and a favorite destination for couples and lovers of a luxurious atmosphere.

The windmills

Old stone mills on the hill between Bodrum and Gümbet, and the best spot for a panoramic photo of the two bays at sunset.

Site of the Mausoleum at Halicarnassus

The site of one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. Today only the foundations and a few stones remain, along with a small exhibition explaining its history.

The ancient theater

An ancient theater on the hillside above the town, offering a wonderful view of the castle and the marina.

Gümüşlük Bay

A quiet fishing village on the ruins of the ancient city of Myndos, known for its waterside fish restaurants and its sunset.

Göltürkbükü

An upscale bay with beach clubs and wooden platforms over the water, sought out by lovers of a luxurious atmosphere and privacy.

Bitez Beach

A calm bay with shallow water and tangerine orchards, suitable for families and for windsurfing.

Boat trips between bays and islands

A day trip on a traditional wooden boat that stops at nearby bays and islands, such as Orak Island, for swimming, with lunch on board.

Day Trips

Tours and day trips from Bodrum

  • Daily boat trip: a tour from morning until late afternoon among the bays, with swimming stops and lunch on board. A private boat can be rented for your family.
  • Peninsula tour by car: the windmills, Yalıkavak, Gümüşlük and Turgutreis at sunset, with stops for photos and coffee.
  • Pamukkale (the Cotton Castle): a long day trip to the white limestone terraces and the ancient city of Hierapolis, about 3.5–4 hours away by car.
  • Ephesus: the best-known Roman city in Turkey, with the Library of Celsus and its great theater, about two and a half to three hours away.
  • The Greek island of Kos (optional): a short ferry ride to Kos, but it is inside the Schengen area, and holders of most Arab passports need a valid Schengen visa, and you need to make sure you can return to Turkey on your current visa. We guide you before booking.

Where to stay?

Best areas to stay in Bodrum

  • Central Bodrum: close to the castle, marina, markets and restaurants, suitable for those who want activity and walking, but noisier on summer nights.
  • Yalıkavak: an upscale area around the luxury marina with hotels and villas with sea views, ideal for honeymoons and luxury stays.
  • Göltürkbükü: a quiet, upscale bay with boutique hotels and beach clubs, suitable for couples and those who value privacy.
  • Bitez: a calm bay with shallow water and family restaurants, close to the town center, and suitable for families with children.
  • Torba: complete family resorts and quiet beaches away from the nightlife areas, and slightly closer to the airport than the town center.

OYA tips

Tips before traveling to Bodrum

  • Bodrum is open-minded and has lively nightlife areas such as Gümbet and the bar street in the center, so if you are with your family, choose to stay in Bitez, Torba or Yalıkavak.
  • Halal resorts are few in Bodrum compared with Antalya, so if a separate pool for women is an essential requirement, Antalya or Alanya may be a better choice.
  • Halal food is available in Turkish restaurants, while in international restaurants, ask about the ingredients before ordering.
  • Summer winds soften the heat but may make the sea choppy in some bays, so choose a calm day for a boat trip with children.
  • The roads between the peninsula's bays are winding and busy in summer, and a private English-speaking driver saves you the search for parking and taxis.
  • Book yachts and private boats early for July and August, and check what the price includes in terms of meals, fuel and route.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about visiting Bodrum

Is Bodrum suitable for families?

Yes, if you choose the right area: Bitez, Torba and Yalıkavak are quiet and have family resorts with private beaches, while Gümbet and the town center lean toward nightlife in the evening. We suggest hotels that suit your family's style.

What is the best time to visit Bodrum?

June to September for swimming and boat trips, while May and October have milder heat and fewer crowds. In winter the town becomes much quieter and a number of seasonal resorts close.

Are there direct flights from the Gulf to Bodrum?

In summer, seasonal direct flights operate from some Gulf cities, and their schedules change from one season to the next. In any case, it is easy to get there via Istanbul on a domestic flight of about an hour and a quarter. We look for the most suitable option for you through our flight ticket service.

Bodrum or Antalya: which is better?

Antalya suits families looking for large all-inclusive resorts, halal resorts and a variety of tours, while Bodrum suits those who love marinas, yachts, quiet bays and an upscale character. Also read the Antalya travel guide.

How much does it cost to rent a yacht in Bodrum?

It depends on the size of the yacht or boat, the number of hours, the season and what is included, such as meals and fuel, and the difference between a shared boat and a large private yacht is very big. Send us the number of people and the date, and we will send you updated offers.

What are the best-known sights in Bodrum?

Bodrum Castle and the Museum of Underwater Archaeology, Bodrum Marina and Yalıkavak, the windmills, the ancient theater, the site of the Mausoleum, the bays of Gümüşlük, Bitez and Göltürkbükü, and boat trips between the islands.

Can I visit Pamukkale or Ephesus from Bodrum?

Yes, both are long day trips by car: Ephesus is about two and a half to three hours away, and Pamukkale about 3.5–4 hours. We recommend leaving early and traveling with a private driver for families.
