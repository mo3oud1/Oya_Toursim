Residency & Approvals

Tourist residence permit in Turkey: file preparation and follow-up

The tourist residence permit is a short-term residence permit granted by the Turkish Presidency of Migration Management to those who want to stay in Turkey longer than their visa or visa-exemption period. We help you prepare the file and apply; the decision rests with the authority alone.

At a glance

Residence applications are submitted online through the migration authority's e-İkamet system, and the application ends with an appointment at the migration directorate of the province where you live, at which you hand in your original documents.

The most common causes of delay and refusal are gaps in the file: an address whose registration is not accepted, insurance that does not cover the requested period, or a passport that expires soon. That is why we review your case first and tell you frankly what is missing before you pay any fees.

What the service includes

  • Reviewing your case and documents before starting.
  • Filling in the online application and booking the appointment.
  • A clear list of the documents required for your case.
  • Help with health insurance suited to the length of the permit.
  • Following up the application and informing you of any additional request from the authority.

Documents

Documents usually required

Passport

Valid, and its validity must exceed the requested residence period by 60 days.

Biometric photos

Four recent photos with a white background.

Health insurance

Valid in Turkey and covering the requested residence period.

Proof of address

A notarized rental contract or a title deed, in a neighborhood where registration of foreigners is accepted.

Proof of income

A statement of financial means for the residence period; the authority may request a bank statement.

Official fee

The residence fee and the card fee according to the annual tariff, paid to the state.

Questions & answers

FAQ

Does a tourist residence permit give the right to work?

No. A tourist residence permit does not allow working in Turkey; work requires a separate work permit.

How long does a tourist residence permit last?

The migration authority sets it for each application according to nationality and file, and it may be shorter than the period you requested.

Can a tourist residence permit be renewed?

The extension application is submitted before the permit expires, and its acceptance is at the discretion of the migration authority and subject to its conditions in force at the time of application. We review your case and tell you frankly what your chances are.

Do you guarantee that I will get the residence permit?

No one can guarantee that; the decision rests with the migration authority alone. What we guarantee is a complete, well-organized file and serious follow-up.
