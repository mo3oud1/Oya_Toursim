Antalya 7-Day Tour Program: Sea, Waterfalls and Mountains

A week on the Turkish Riviera: a seaside resort, the Old Town and Düden Waterfalls, the Olympos cable car, Side and Manavgat, and Alanya Castle.

Antalya → Kemer → Side → AlanyaBeachesFamilyHoneymoon
Book this programThe program day by day

About the program

The Antalya 7-Day Tour Program combines the comfort of a resort on the Mediterranean with tours that make the trip more than just a beach: the Kaleiçi old town, the Düden Waterfalls that fall into the sea, the Olympos cable car to the summit of Tahtalı Mountain, the Roman theatres of Aspendos and Side, Alanya Castle and Damlataş Cave. We also left one whole day free for the pool and the sea.

You stay six nights in a seaside resort or hotel in Lara, Konyaaltı or Belek, on a breakfast or all-inclusive basis of your choice, and conservative (halal) hotels are available, with pools and separate women's beaches in some areas. The driver meets you at Antalya Airport, and the tours are by private car with an English-speaking driver. You can reach Antalya on a direct flight from several Arab cities in season, or on a domestic flight from Istanbul that we book for you in the same request.

Trip highlights

  • 6 nights in a seaside resort on a breakfast or all-inclusive basis
  • Kaleiçi, Hadrian's Gate and the boat trip to the Lower Düden Waterfall
  • Olympos cable car to the summit of Tahtalı Mountain and the Kemer beaches
  • Roman theatre of Aspendos, the city of Side and Manavgat Waterfall
  • Alanya Castle, Damlataş Cave and Cleopatra Beach
  • A free day for the sea and the pool, or for the theme park

The program day by day

  1. Day1

    Arrival in Antalya

    The driver meets you at Antalya Airport (AYT) and takes you to the resort, a drive of about 20 to 40 minutes depending on the area. After check-in, the rest of the day is for relaxing on the beach or by the pool. We hand you your SIM card and go over the tour program with you.

    • Antalya Airport (AYT)
    • Resort check-in
    • Beach or pool
  2. Day2

    The old town and Düden Waterfalls

    We start in the old Kaleiçi district: the Roman Hadrian's Gate, the Fluted Minaret, and the lanes with Ottoman houses, down to the old harbor. From there, a boat trip along the coast to see the Lower Düden Waterfall falling from the cliff into the sea (boat trips are seasonal). In the afternoon we visit the Upper Düden Waterfalls in their green park, and finish with the sunset at Konyaaltı Beach.

    • Hadrian's Gate
    • The Fluted Minaret
    • Kaleiçi district
    • The old harbor
    • Boat trip to the Lower Düden Waterfall
    • Upper Düden Waterfalls
    • Konyaaltı Beach
  3. Day3

    Kemer and the Olympos cable car

    We head southwest along the coast to Kemer and ride the Olympos cable car to the summit of Tahtalı Mountain at over 2,300 meters, with views of the sea and mountains and sometimes snow until early summer. After coming down we visit the ancient city of Phaselis among forests and small bays. We finish at Moonlight Park and the beach in Kemer before heading back.

    • Olympos cable car (Tahtalı Mountain)
    • Ancient city of Phaselis
    • Moonlight Park in Kemer
    • Kemer Marina
  4. Day4

    Free day for the sea and the resort

    A full day to relax on the beach or by the pool and enjoy the resort facilities. If you want an extra activity, we suggest the Antalya Aquarium in Konyaaltı, the Land of Legends theme park in Belek, or an evening of shopping at one of the city's malls. A half-day car can be booked on request.

    • The resort and the beach
    • Antalya Aquarium (optional)
    • Land of Legends in Belek (optional)
    • Evening shopping (optional)
  5. Day5

    Aspendos, Side and Manavgat

    We head east to the Aspendos Theatre, one of the best-preserved Roman theatres in the world. Then we continue to the Manavgat Waterfall, and a boat trip on the Manavgat River to near where it meets the sea (optional). We finish in the ancient city of Side, between the Temple of Apollo overlooking the sea, its ancient theatre and its markets.

    • Aspendos Theatre
    • Manavgat Waterfall
    • Boat trip on the Manavgat River (optional)
    • Temple of Apollo in Side
    • Ancient theatre of Side
  6. Day6

    Alanya: castle, cave and beach

    We set off for Alanya, about two hours away, and go up by cable car or by car to Alanya Castle on the rock above the sea. We visit Damlataş Cave with its limestone formations, then Cleopatra Beach with its clear water. In season we have lunch on the cool platforms over the Dim River before returning to the resort.

    • Alanya Castle cable car
    • Alanya Castle
    • Damlataş Cave
    • Cleopatra Beach
    • Dim River (in season)
  7. Day7

    Airport transfer in Antalya

    A last morning by the sea, depending on your flight time. The driver picks you up at the resort and takes you to Antalya Airport with plenty of time before departure. We wish you a safe journey home.

    • Resort check-out
    • Antalya Airport (AYT)

Included in the program

  • Private-car airport pickup and drop-off at Antalya Airport (AYT)
  • 6 nights' accommodation in a 4–5-star seaside resort or hotel with breakfast (upgrade to all-inclusive available)
  • 4 full-day tours in a modern car with an English-speaking driver (up to 10 hours a day)
  • Fuel, parking and road fees
  • Turkish SIM card with a data plan (one per booking)
  • Follow-up from the OYA team on WhatsApp throughout the trip

Not included

  • International or domestic flight tickets to Antalya (we book them for you on request)
  • Turkey entry visa for nationalities that require one — Do you need a visa?
  • Entrance fees to archaeological sites and museums such as Aspendos, Side, Phaselis, Alanya Castle and Damlataş Cave
  • The Olympos and Alanya cable cars and boat trips
  • Lunches and dinners (unless you choose all-inclusive)
  • Optional activities such as parasailing, Antalya Aquarium and Land of Legends
  • Travel health insurance, tips and personal expenses

Questions & answers

Questions about the Antalya 7-Day Program

How long is the flight from Istanbul to Antalya?

About an hour and a quarter to an hour and a half, with many daily flights from Istanbul Airport and Sabiha Gökçen. Direct flights from several Arab cities also reach Antalya, especially in summer.

What is the best area to stay in Antalya for families?

Lara and Belek suit those who want large all-inclusive resorts with children's facilities, Konyaaltı is close to the city and the long beach, and Alanya and Side have more conservative hotel options.

What does all-inclusive mean?

It means that the three meals, drinks and snacks inside the resort are included in the price of the stay. What it covers varies from resort to resort, so we give you the details in the offer.

Can Antalya be combined with Istanbul or other cities?

Yes, you can add days in Istanbul before or after Antalya, with a short domestic flight. For a trip covering Antalya, Cappadocia and Trabzon together, see the Turkey Complete 14-Day Program.
