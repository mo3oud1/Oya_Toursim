10-Day Black Sea Tour: Trabzon, Uzungöl and Ayder

Ten days between the Black Sea coast and its plateaus: Trabzon, Uzungöl, Sultan Murat, Ayder and Rize, Sümela Monastery and Karaca Cave, at a relaxed pace.

Trabzon → Uzungöl → Rize → AyderNatureFamilyFriends

About the program

The 10-Day Black Sea Tour is the complete trip for nature lovers: three main areas of Turkey's Black Sea region, Trabzon, Uzungöl and Ayder in Rize, with high plateaus, caves, lakes and tea plantations. The length lets you visit all the well-known stops without spending your days on the road, with two days for shopping and rest in Trabzon.

The tour starts and ends at Trabzon Airport, which you can reach on a seasonal direct flight or via Istanbul. You spend 3 nights in Trabzon, then 2 nights on Lake Uzungöl, 1 night in Ayder in the Kaçkar Mountains, and the last 3 nights in Trabzon. All tours are by private car with an English-speaking driver who knows the mountain roads well, and day 9 can become a trip to Batumi in Georgia for travelers whose nationality allows it. If you have less time, see the 7-day Trabzon and Uzungöl tour, a shorter version of this trip.

Trip highlights

  • Trabzon: Hagia Sophia, Atatürk Mansion, Lake Sera and Boztepe Hill
  • Sümela Monastery and the village of Hamsiköy in the Altındere Valley
  • Two nights on Lake Uzungöl with Sultan Murat Plateau
  • One night in Ayder with the Gelin Tülü waterfall, Zil Castle and the Fırtına Valley
  • Tea plantations in Rize and the tea garden overlooking the city
  • Hıdırnebi Plateau, Karaca Cave and the Torul Glass Terrace
  • Option of a day trip to Batumi in Georgia

The program day by day

  1. Day1

    Arrival in Trabzon

    We meet you at Trabzon Airport (TZX), close to the city center, and take you to your hotel to rest. Before sunset we go up Boztepe Hill for views of the city and the Black Sea, with a cup of tea. We hand you your SIM card and go over the program with you.

    • Trabzon Airport (TZX)
    • Hotel check-in
    • Boztepe Hill
  2. Day2

    Trabzon City and Lake Sera

    We start with Hagia Sophia of Trabzon, the Byzantine church that became a mosque and still keeps its frescoes. Next come the Atatürk Mansion and its garden in the Soğuksu district, then Lake Sera west of the city for a walk around the lake. We have lunch in Akçaabat, famous for its köfte, and return in the evening to Meydan Square and Uzun Sokak Street.

    • Hagia Sophia of Trabzon
    • Atatürk Palace
    • Lake Sera
    • Akçaabat
    • Meydan Square and Uzun Sokak Street
  3. Day3

    Sümela Monastery and Hamsiköy

    We set off for Maçka and the Altındere Valley, about an hour away, to visit Sümela Monastery, carved into a cliff above the forests; reaching it takes a few minutes of uphill walking. Then we head up to the village of Hamsiköy to taste rice pudding with hazelnuts in its cafes overlooking the valley. We return to Trabzon in the afternoon with time to rest or shop.

    • Maçka
    • Altındere Valley
    • Sümela Monastery
    • Hamsiköy village
  4. Day4

    The road to Uzungöl

    We head east along the coast, then uphill to Uzungöl, about two hours with stops. We stop in Sürmene for its knife shops, at a tea house, and at the waterfalls in the Çaykara Valley. After check-in we stroll around the lake and its mosque, and visit a horse farm and the honey and Turkish delight shops.

    • Sürmene
    • Tea shop or factory
    • Çaykara Valley waterfalls
    • Lake Uzungöl
    • Horse farm
    • Honey and Turkish delight shops
  5. Day5

    Sultan Murat Plateau and the Uzungöl viewpoint

    We drive up to Sultan Murat Plateau, about 35 kilometers away on a mountain road, with its green pastures, stone houses and clouds drifting between the peaks. We return to visit the panoramic Uzungöl viewpoint above the lake, with the option of trying the zipline. The evening is free on the lakeshore.

    • Sultan Murat Plateau
    • Panoramic Uzungöl viewpoint
    • Zip line (optional)
    • Uzungöl lakeshore
  6. Day6

    From Uzungöl to Rize and Ayder

    We leave Uzungöl for Rize and stop at the tea garden overlooking the city, among the tea plantations on the slopes. Then we enter the Fırtına Valley and climb to Ayder Plateau; the whole drive takes about two and a half to three hours. We visit the Gelin Tülü waterfall, and you can try the hot springs before the overnight stay in a hotel or wooden cabin.

    • Tea garden in Rize
    • Tea plantations
    • Fırtına Valley
    • Ayder Plateau
    • Gelintülü Waterfall
    • Hot springs in Ayder (optional)
  7. Day7

    Zil Castle, Fırtına Valley and back to Trabzon

    After breakfast you can take a local 4x4 up to Hoser Plateau above Ayder (optional). Then we visit Zil Castle on a rock above the Fırtına Valley and stop at the Ottoman stone bridges, with the option of rafting or ziplining in season. In the afternoon we return to Trabzon, about three hours.

    • Hoser Plateau (optional)
    • Zil Castle
    • Stone bridges in the Fırtına Valley
    • Rafting (optional, in season)
    • Back to Trabzon
  8. Day8

    Hıdırnebi Plateau and shopping

    A morning at Hıdırnebi Plateau, one of the highlands near Trabzon that visitors love, with its meadows and mountain views, and the option of a barbecue or a village breakfast. In the afternoon we return to the city to shop at Forum Trabzon or the Kunduracılar Bazaar, where you will find tea, hazelnuts, honey and textiles. The evening is free to rest.

    • Haydarnabi Plateau
    • Forum Trabzon
    • Kunduracılar bazaar
  9. Day9

    Zigana, Torul Glass Terrace and Karaca Cave

    We head south on the Gümüşhane road through Zigana Pass and its plateaus, then stop at the Torul Glass Terrace above the valley. We continue to Karaca Cave with its colorful stalagmites and stalactites, and return to Trabzon in the evening. Alternatively, this day can become a trip to Batumi in Georgia (about three hours each way via the Sarp border crossing), depending on your nationality, or a free day.

    • Zigana Pass
    • Torul Glass Terrace
    • Karaca Cave
    • Alternative: Batumi trip in Georgia
  10. Day10

    Farewell at Trabzon Airport

    A free morning for last-minute shopping, depending on your flight time. The driver picks you up at the hotel and takes you to Trabzon Airport with plenty of time before takeoff. We wish you a safe journey home.

    • Free time for shopping
    • Hotel check-out
    • Trabzon Airport (TZX)

Questions & answers

Questions about the 10-Day Black Sea Tour

What is Turkey's Black Sea region and what are its main cities?

The name for the Black Sea region in northeast Turkey. Its main stops are Trabzon, Uzungöl, Rize and Ayder Plateau, plus high plateaus such as Sultan Murat and Hıdırnebi.

How far is Ayder from Uzungöl?

About 150 to 160 kilometers, roughly two and a half to three hours by car via the city of Rize. That is why we included a full night in Ayder instead of going there and back in one day.

Can I visit Batumi in Georgia from Trabzon?

Yes. Batumi is about three hours by car via the Sarp border crossing, and day 9 can become a trip there, depending on your nationality and Georgia's entry requirements. For a longer visit, see the 7-day Georgia tour.

Is the tour suitable for older travelers and children?

Yes. The longest driving day is about three hours, and sightseeing needs little effort, except Sümela Monastery, which involves a short uphill walk. Any day can be swapped for a rest day.

Is the tour suitable in winter?

Winter is beautiful, with snow in Uzungöl, but some roads and high plateaus may close, so we suggest a modified version then. If snow is what you want, also see the Istanbul and Uludağ winter tour.
