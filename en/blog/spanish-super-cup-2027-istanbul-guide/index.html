Spanish Super Cup 2027 in Istanbul: A Fan's Guide to Dates, Stay and Transport

Everything a fan needs to attend the Spanish Super Cup 2027 in Istanbul: the announced dates, choosing a hotel, getting around on match day, and combining the tournament with a winter trip.

For the first time, fans of Spanish football are turning their eyes to Turkey, as Istanbul hosts the Spanish Super Cup 2027 as announced, with Barcelona, Atlético Madrid, Real Madrid and Real Sociedad taking part. If you are planning to attend, this guide gathers what you need: the announced dates, choosing where to stay, getting around on match day, and how to turn a football trip into a full winter holiday.

We would point out from the start that tournament details come from the organizing body, and dates, stadiums or kick-off times may change. So we keep checking for updates and confirm them with you when you book.

Spanish Super Cup 2027 dates in Istanbul

The tournament is played in a four-team format: two semi-finals and then a final. These are the dates as announced up to the date this article was published:

MatchThe two sidesDate and stadium
First semi-finalBarcelona × Atlético MadridTuesday, February 2, 2027 • 22:00 — Şükrü Saracoğlu Stadium (Kadıköy)
Second semi-finalReal Sociedad × Real MadridWednesday, February 3, 2027 • 22:00 — Tüpraş Stadyumu (Beşiktaş)
FinalThe winners of the semi-finalsSaturday, February 6, 2027 • 22:00 — RAMS Park

Matches kick off at 22:00 Istanbul time and end around midnight, which is why we advise booking an extra night after each match you want to attend, especially the night of the final, so that you are not tied to a flight in a tight window.

Why did the tournament move to Istanbul?

Saudi Arabia has hosted many editions of the Spanish Super Cup in recent years, but the 2027 edition coincides with the Kingdom hosting the 2027 Asian Cup. That is why the Super Cup was moved to Istanbul this time, as announced, a city used to hosting major European matches and with a wide network of transport and hotels.

Where are the matches played?

Each match has its own stadium: the first semi-final in Kadıköy on the Asian side, the second in Beşiktaş, and the final at RAMS Park in the north of the city. Choose where to stay according to the match you will attend, or a central area close to the main metro lines if you will attend more than one match.

Follow our Spanish Super Cup 2027 tickets page, as we update it whenever there is news about the tournament.

Tickets: how do you get them safely?

  • Buy from a trusted source: The official channels of the organizing body, or a well-known travel agency that deals openly. Avoid unknown sellers on social media.
  • Read the ticket terms: Some tickets are issued in the holder's name and require you to show your passport or ID at entry.
  • Do not book travel before the picture is clear: Tie your flight and hotel bookings to ticket confirmation as far as you can.
  • Mind your seat location: Each team's fan stands are usually separate, so choose the seat that suits your allegiance.

We are not an official body affiliated with the Spanish federation or any club. You can browse our other services in the match tickets section.

Where to stay in Istanbul during the tournament

The golden rule: stay near a metro station on a main line, not just near a landmark. Istanbul's traffic on match-day evenings can make a car journey very long, while the metro remains faster and more reliable.

AreaWhy does it suit fans?Note
Kadıköy and ÜsküdarClosest to the first semi-final stadium on the Asian side, with ferries to the European sideBest for those attending the Barcelona v Atlético match
Şişli and MecidiyeköyA transport hub combining the metro and the metrobus, close to shopping centersA practical choice for those attending more than one match
TaksimThe heart of the city, restaurants, cafes and a direct metroCrowded in the evening, and suits those who like a lively atmosphere
BeşiktaşBosphorus views, ferries and busesLovely for families and couples with a sightseeing program
SultanahmetNext to Hagia Sophia and the Blue Mosque, and connected by tramBest for those who put sightseeing tours first

We expect hotel demand to rise during tournament week as fans arrive from Spain and abroad, so it is advisable to book early with a flexible cancellation policy. We recommend the option that suits your group size and budget through our hotel booking service.

Getting around on match day

  1. Istanbulkart: Top it up in advance; it works on the metro, tram, metrobus, ferries and buses.
  2. Set out early: Allow two to three hours before kick-off to arrive and get through security checks.
  3. Plan your return: Leaving the stadium is slow, and transport gets crowded after the final whistle. Agree a clear meeting point with your group.
  4. Private transport: For families and older travelers, we arrange a private car with a driver waiting at an agreed point, taking into account the roads closed around the stadium.
  5. Carry only what you need: Your passport or ID, and your ticket on your phone with enough charge. Avoid large bags, as they may be refused.

The weather in Istanbul in early February

February is one of Istanbul's coldest months, with temperatures often ranging from a few degrees above zero to around ten degrees, with frequent rain and a chance of snow in some years. Open stands make it feel colder in the evening, so bring a warm coat, gloves and a scarf, and a small umbrella for getting around the city.

Combine the match with a winter sightseeing trip

The tournament days are separated by enough time to explore the city and its surroundings. Here is a suggested program for those who want to attend the whole tournament:

DaySuggested program
February 1Arrival and airport pickup, then a light evening walk
February 2Old Istanbul tour in the morning, then the first semi-final in the evening
February 3A Bosphorus tour by car or boat, then the second semi-final
February 4Day trip to Bursa and Mount Uludağ to enjoy the snow
February 5Sapanca and Maşukiye, or a free shopping day
February 6A quiet day, then the final match at the confirmed time
February 7Transfer to the airport, or an extra day for shopping and tours

If you prefer snow and nature, the Istanbul and Uludağ winter 6-day program suits the timing of the tournament perfectly, and can be adjusted to include one or more matches.

Important reminder: Ramadan 1448 begins on approximately February 8, 2027, just a few days after the final. If you would rather be back home before it starts, book your return flight early, because demand rises in that period.

Quick tips for fans coming from abroad

  • Book your flight early: fares to Istanbul during tournament week may rise as the date approaches, and direct options are available from most Arab cities. Start from the flight booking page and we will compare the options for you.
  • Choose the right airport: Istanbul Airport is on the European side and Sabiha Gökçen is on the Asian side, with a long distance between them, so pick the one closest to your hotel.
  • Set a budget for daily expenses: restaurants near the stadiums and tourist areas are usually pricier than those in residential neighborhoods.
  • For families with children: consider attending just one match and keeping the other days for sightseeing, as evening matches in the cold are tiring for little ones.
  • Respect the stadium atmosphere: cheer for your team with passion, avoid friction with the other team's fans, and follow the security instructions.

Questions & answers

Related questions

When are the Spanish Super Cup 2027 matches held in Istanbul?

As announced: the first semi-final (Barcelona v Atlético Madrid) on Tuesday, February 2, 2027, the second semi-final (Real Sociedad v Real Madrid) on Wednesday, February 3, and the final on Saturday, February 6. All matches are at 22:00 Istanbul time.

Why was the tournament not held in Saudi Arabia this time?

Because its date coincides with Saudi Arabia hosting the 2027 Asian Cup, so the 2027 edition was moved to Istanbul, as announced.

Is OYA an official seller of Spanish Super Cup tickets?

No, we are not an official body affiliated with the Spanish federation or the clubs. We do not sell tickets: we help you buy them from the official channel and in your own name, and we arrange your hotel and transfers.

What is the best area to stay in for the matches?

Choose an area close to a main metro line, such as Şişli and Mecidiyeköy, or Taksim, and we will pick the most suitable one after the stadium is officially confirmed. If sightseeing is your priority, you can stay in Sultanahmet.

Can I combine the match with a trip to the snow?

Yes. Early February is usually snow season on Mount Uludağ, and you can add a day trip to Bursa and Uludağ or to Sapanca between the semi-finals and the final.
