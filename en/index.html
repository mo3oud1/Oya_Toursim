Questions & answers

FAQ

Does your team speak English?

Yes, the OYA Tourism team communicates with you in English from the first message until your return, and our tour programs include English-speaking drivers so your trip is comfortable with no language barrier.

How do I book with you?

Send us your destination, dates and number of travelers via WhatsApp or the booking form on the site, and we will send you a clear written quote with the details. After you approve, we confirm the bookings and explain the available payment methods and the cancellation policy before any payment.

Do you offer pickup from Istanbul Airport and a private driver?

Yes, we arrange pickup from Istanbul Airport (IST), Sabiha Gökçen Airport (SAW) and all Turkish airports, with a private car and an English-speaking driver for transfers and tours.

Can a special program be designed for my family?

Absolutely. All our programs can be adjusted: number of days, cities, hotel category and car type. Tell us the children's ages and your interests and we will design a program that suits you.

Can I book while I am outside Turkey?

Yes, you can complete everything remotely via WhatsApp and email: hotel, program, flights and visa, and you receive written confirmations before you travel.

Do you help with visas even if I haven't booked a trip with you?

Yes, the visa service is independent: we help with Saudi visas of all types, Gulf country visas, and Schengen, UK, US and Canada visas. Please note that the decision to grant a visa rests with the official authorities.
