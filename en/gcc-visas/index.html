Why apply through OYA?

One mistake in a letter or a number… could cost you the entire visa fee

An application can be rejected, or its fees lost, because of one small mistake: a letter in the name that does not match the passport, a missing digit, a reversed date, or a photo in the wrong size. Government fees are usually non-refundable. That is why we submit your Gulf visa application for you and put your file in order from the start.

Apply through us
  • We fill in the application for youand match every letter and number to your passport before submitting.
  • We review photos and documentsagainst the official specifications, so the file is not rejected over a formality.
  • We organize your file correctlyand complete the bookings and paperwork required for your type of visa.
  • We follow your application until the decisionand send you the e-visa on WhatsApp as soon as it is issued.

OYA Tourism is a private travel agency, not a government body. Our service fee is modest, and we explain it to you separately from the official fees before we start; the final decision rests with the competent authority alone.

Qatar Visa

You may not need a Qatar visa at all: many nationalities, including Turkish citizens, enter Qatar without a prior visa, while others apply through the Hayya platform. At OYA Tourism we work out the right route for you and prepare your application in full.

Official platform: Hayya platformVisa exemption: for more than 100 nationalities

OM

Oman Visa

The Oman e-Visa is issued through the official portal of the Royal Oman Police, and many nationalities, including Turkish citizens, can enter the Sultanate without a visa for up to 14 days. At OYA Tourism we work out the best option for you and prepare your file and bookings.

Visa-free entry: up to 14 days for eligible nationalitiesOfficial platform: Royal Oman Police e-Visa portal
Details and requirements
BH

Bahrain Visa

The Bahrain e-Visa is requested through the official portal of the Government of Bahrain, and is available with validity from two weeks up to 5 years. At OYA Tourism we choose the right type with you, prepare your file and follow it up until a decision is issued.

Entry type: single or multiple entryValidity: from two weeks to 5 years, depending on the type
Details and requirements
KW

Kuwait Visa

Applying for a Kuwait visa has been fully electronic since the Ministry of Interior launched the “Kuwait Visa” platform in July 2025, which issues tourist, family and business visas. At OYA Tourism we prepare and follow up your application, and we review the updated requirements before every submission.

Official platform: Kuwait Visa platform – Ministry of InteriorLength of stay: up to 30 days per entry

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about Gulf visas

How long does a Dubai visa take?

UAE tourist visas are usually issued within a few working days once the documents are complete, and the time may vary by nationality and season. We give you an accurate estimate when we open your application.

Can a Syrian or Iraqi living in Turkey get a Dubai visa?

Applications are open to many nationalities, but the requirements and chances of approval vary by nationality and profile. Send us a photo of your passport and your residence type, and we will assess the application before it is submitted.

What is the difference between a 30-day and a 60-day visa?

The difference is the length of stay allowed in the country, and single-entry and multiple-entry options are available. We choose the right type with you for the length of your trip.

When does the unified GCC visa start?

Follow our unified GCC visa page for the latest official announcements. We notify everyone registered with us as soon as applications officially open.

Do you guarantee approval?

No. The decision rests with the official authorities in each country. Our role is to prepare the application correctly, follow it up and let you know the result as soon as it is issued.
