Istanbul, Sapanca and Bursa 8-day tour program with overnight stays in bungalows

Three destinations in one trip: Istanbul's landmarks, then two nights between Lake Sapanca and the forests of Maşukiye, and a night in Bursa with Mount Uludağ.

Istanbul → Sapanca → BursaFamilyNatureCities

About the program

The Istanbul, Sapanca and Bursa 8-Day Tour is the right choice if you want the city and nature together in one trip. You begin with three nights in Istanbul for the old city and the Bosphorus, then two nights in a bungalow or chalet near Lake Sapanca among the forests, then one night in Bursa to go up Mount Uludağ, before returning to Istanbul for a last night.

We designed the route as a loop so you do not go back the same way: from Istanbul east to Sapanca in about two hours, then to Bursa in two to two and a half hours, and from there to Istanbul over the Osmangazi Bridge. In Sapanca we choose a bungalow with a Jacuzzi or a villa with a private pool for you, depending on the season and budget, which makes the program popular with families and honeymooners. All transfers are by private car with an English-speaking driver, from the airport pickup to the farewell at the end of the trip.

Trip highlights

  • 3 nights in Istanbul: the old city, the Bosphorus and the Asian side
  • Two nights in Sapanca bungalows, with a choice of Jacuzzi or private pool
  • Maşukiye waterfalls and the Kartepe cable car above the forests
  • Lake Sapanca: a walk, a boat ride and optional adventure activities
  • Historic Bursa: the Grand Mosque, Silk Bazaar and Green Mosque
  • Uludağ cable car and the ancient plane tree in Inkaya
  • A comfortable loop route with no backtracking

The program day by day

  1. Day1

    Arrival in Istanbul

    Your driver meets you at Istanbul Airport (IST) or Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) and takes you to the hotel. After a rest, we suggest a light evening outing around Taksim Square and İstiklal Avenue or in the Galata area. We hand you a SIM card and go over the program with you.

    • Istanbul Airport or Sabiha Gökçen
    • Hotel check-in
    • Taksim and İstiklal Avenue
  2. Day2

    Old city: Sultanahmet and the Grand Bazaar

    A full day on the historic peninsula: the Blue Mosque, the Hippodrome square and Hagia Sophia, then the Basilica Cistern and Topkapı Palace. In the afternoon we visit the Grand Bazaar with its covered lanes, then the Spice Bazaar in Eminönü. We finish with a short walk on the Galata Bridge at sunset.

    • Blue Mosque
    • Hagia Sophia
    • Basilica Cistern
    • Topkapı Palace
    • Grand Bazaar
    • Spice Bazaar
    • Galata Bridge
  3. Day3

    The Bosphorus, Maiden's Tower and Çamlıca

    We start with Dolmabahçe Palace, then Ortaköy and Bebek on the European shore, and take a Bosphorus boat cruise of about an hour and a half. After that we cross to Üsküdar on the Asian side to see Maiden's Tower from the shore, then go up to the Çamlıca Mosque and Hill to see the city at sunset. You can end the day with an optional seafood dinner.

    • Dolmabahçe Palace
    • Ortaköy
    • Bebek
    • Bosphorus boat tour
    • Üsküdar and Maiden's Tower
    • Çamlıca Mosque and Hill
  4. Day4

    Transfer to Sapanca via Maşukiye

    After breakfast and check-out, we head east toward Sapanca, about two hours away. We stop in the village of Maşukiye on the slopes of Mount Kartepe, with its waterfalls, streams and restaurants among the trees, and ride the Kartepe cable car for views of the forests and the lake from above. In the afternoon we reach the bungalow in Sapanca, and the evening is yours for relaxing, a barbecue or the Jacuzzi.

    • Maşukiye Village
    • Maşukiye waterfalls
    • Kartepe Cable Car
    • Check-in at the Sapanca bungalows
  5. Day5

    Sapanca Lake day and activities

    A calm morning on the shores of Lake Sapanca, with a walk along the promenade and a boat ride. In the afternoon you can try the optional activities: ATV rides in the forest, zip-lining or horseback riding. For those with children, we suggest a visit to Ormanya Wildlife Park in Kocaeli, about 45 minutes away, and then back to the bungalow.

    • Lake Sapanca promenade
    • Boat ride on the lake
    • ATV and zipline activities (optional)
    • Ormanya Wildlife Park (optional)
  6. Day6

    From Sapanca to Bursa and a city tour

    We head to Bursa, a drive of two to two and a half hours along the Gulf of İzmit. We visit the Grand Mosque, the Silk Bazaar at Koza Han and the Covered Bazaar, then the Green Mosque and the tombs of Osman Gazi and Orhan Gazi. After check-in, you can try the hot-spring baths in the Çekirge district in the evening, and taste the İskender kebab that Bursa is famous for.

    • The Grand Mosque (Ulu Cami)
    • Koza Han (the Silk Bazaar)
    • Covered Bazaar
    • Green Mosque
    • Tombs of Osman Gazi and Orhan Gazi
    • Çekirge district
  7. Day7

    Uludağ cable car and return to Istanbul

    In the morning we ride the Uludağ cable car up to the heights to enjoy the snow in winter, or the meadows and fresh air in summer. On the way down we have tea under the ancient plane tree in Inkaya, then head to Istanbul, about two and a half hours over the Osmangazi Bridge. You reach the hotel in the evening, with time for dinner and a walk.

    • Uludağ Cable Car
    • Uludağ heights
    • The Plane Tree at İnkaya
    • Osmangazi Bridge
    • Hotel in Istanbul
  8. Day8

    Shopping and farewell at the airport

    Depending on your flight time, you can shop in the morning at one of the big malls or buy gifts at the markets near the hotel. Your driver picks you up at the agreed time and takes you to the airport about three hours before departure. We wish you a safe journey home.

    • Free time for shopping
    • Hotel check-out
    • Transfer to the airport

Questions & answers

Questions about the Istanbul, Sapanca and Bursa 8-Day Tour

How far is Sapanca from Istanbul?

Sapanca is about 140 kilometers from central Istanbul, roughly two hours by car depending on traffic, and it is closer to Sabiha Gökçen Airport on the Asian side.

What is the difference between Sapanca and Maşukiye?

Sapanca (also spelled Sabanca or Sapancha) is a town on a large lake, known for its bungalows and villas. Maşukiye is a nearby mountain village on the slopes of Kartepe, known for its waterfalls and restaurants among the trees. On this program you visit both.

Do the bungalows have a private pool?

We offer several options: bungalows with an indoor Jacuzzi, villas with a private pool, or hotels on the lake. We confirm the details and photos of the unit with you before you confirm the booking.

Is the program suitable for children?

Yes. The distances between cities are moderate (about two hours), and there are activities for children such as the boat, the wildlife park and snow on Uludağ in winter. We choose a car that suits the size of your family.
