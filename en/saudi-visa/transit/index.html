Saudi Transit Visa – 96 Hours (Stopover)

If your trip from Istanbul to another destination connects through Saudi Arabia on Saudia or flynas, you can request a Saudi transit visa and stop in the Kingdom for up to 96 hours. We book your flight on the right route and help you apply for the visa.

Overview

Saudi regulations allow government transit visas of 48 or 96 hours. Alongside them, the stopover program with Saudia and flynas offers an e-visa for stopping in the Kingdom for up to 96 hours during a connecting trip, requested as part of the ticket booking steps themselves.

The visa is requested during booking on the airline's website and sent to your email after approval, and it is valid for about 3 months from issue. According to the official program page there is no government visa fee, but a small processing and insurance fee may be added, which we confirm with you at booking.

Some sources indicate that the transit visa allows you to visit Makkah and Madinah and perform Umrah after booking a permit in the Nusuk app, which makes it an economical option for those passing through the Kingdom. However, we verify this and the availability of a permit before booking, because the conditions are updated from time to time.

A practical example: a trip from Istanbul to a destination in Asia or Africa via Jeddah or Riyadh, with a stop of three or four days to see the city or perform Umrah. At OYA Tourism we design the right flight route and book your stopover hotel, while the decision to grant the visa rests with the Saudi authorities.

Who can apply?

  • Travelers on Saudia or flynas on a transit trip through the Kingdom to a third destination.
  • Those who want to use the stopover to visit Jeddah, Riyadh or other cities in the Kingdom.
  • Those who want to perform Umrah during the stopover, after checking eligibility and the availability of a Nusuk permit.
  • The eligibility conditions shown by the airline at booking apply, and we check them for your nationality.

Types and options

Types of transit visa

Government transit visa

Transit visas of 48 or 96 hours under Saudi regulations. If your trip is not on the airlines taking part in the stopover program, we guide you to the right route.

Stopover visa

Requested as part of your ticket booking on Saudia or flynas, it allows a stop of up to 96 hours, with no government visa fee according to the program page, plus a small processing and insurance fee.

Questions & answers

Transit visa FAQ

What is the 96-hour transit visa, and is it free?

It is an e-visa that lets you stop in Saudi Arabia for up to 96 hours during a connecting trip on Saudia or flynas. According to the program page there is no government visa fee, but a small processing and insurance fee may be added, which we confirm with you at booking.

Can I perform Umrah with the Saudi transit visa?

Some sources say the stopover visa allows you to visit Makkah and Madinah and perform Umrah after booking a permit in the Nusuk app, but we have not found this in an up-to-date official source. So we check whether Umrah is possible and whether a permit is available before booking.

How do I get the Saudi stopover visa?

It is requested while booking your transit ticket on the Saudia or flynas website, then sent to you by email after approval. We can design and book your itinerary and help you with the application step by step.

Is the transit visa valid for a round trip to Saudi Arabia only?

No. It is tied to a transit trip that passes through the Kingdom on the way to a third destination. To visit Saudi Arabia on a round trip, you need the tourist visa or the Umrah visa, depending on your nationality and the purpose of your trip.

Can I get the transit visa if I travel with another airline?

The stopover program is tied to Saudia and flynas. There are also government transit visas of 48 or 96 hours with different procedures, and we guide you to the option available for your trip and nationality.
