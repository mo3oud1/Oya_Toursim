Turkey Honeymoon Program, 9 Days: Istanbul, Cappadocia and Sapanca

Nine days for newlyweds: romantic Istanbul on the Bosphorus, two nights in a cave hotel in Cappadocia with a balloon option, and two nights in a private bungalow in Sapanca.

Istanbul → Cappadocia → SapancaHoneymoonLuxuryNature
Book this programThe program day by day

About the program

The Turkey Honeymoon Program, 9 Days brings together three very different experiences in one trip: Istanbul with its Bosphorus evenings and bazaars, Cappadocia with its cave hotels and sunrise balloons over the valleys, and Sapanca with its quiet bungalows between the forests and the lake. For each stop we chose a setting that suits newlyweds, with enough time to rest and no rushing.

The domestic flights between Istanbul and Cappadocia are included, and all transfers are by private car with a driver, so there is no need for buses or group tours. You can upgrade the trip by adding the balloon flight, a private yacht dinner on the Bosphorus, or a suite with a view, and we arrange it with the hotels in advance. If you prefer Turkey's Black Sea region, you can replace Cappadocia with Uzungöl and Trabzon. The best times for this program are spring and autumn, when the weather is mild in all three places, but it is enjoyable in every season, just with a different atmosphere: snow in Cappadocia in winter and lush greenery in Sapanca in summer. Send us your wedding date and travel dates, and we will design the right offer for you.

Trip highlights

  • Two nights in a cave hotel in Göreme or Uçhisar
  • Sunrise balloon flight in Cappadocia (optional) and Red Valley sunset
  • Two nights in a bungalow with a jacuzzi or a villa with a private pool in Sapanca
  • Bosphorus by boat, Maiden's Tower and Çamlıca sunset
  • Option of a private yacht dinner or a dinner cruise on the Bosphorus
  • Domestic flights included and a private car throughout the trip

The program day by day

  1. Day1

    Arrival in Istanbul

    The driver meets you at Istanbul Airport (IST) or Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) and takes you to the hotel, and we ask the hotel to add a newlywed welcome touch, as far as it can offer. The evening is free to rest, or for an optional seafood dinner on the Bosphorus or on a private yacht. We hand you your SIM card and go over the program with you.

    • Istanbul Airport or Sabiha Gökçen
    • Hotel check-in
    • Seafood dinner on the Bosphorus (optional)
  2. Day2

    The Old City and Galata Tower

    We start with the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia and the Basilica Cistern, then Topkapı Palace and its gardens overlooking the sea. In the afternoon we tour the Grand Bazaar, with its gold, silver and carpet shops, then cross Galata Bridge to the Galata district and climb Galata Tower to see the city at sunset. You can end the day with a Turkish coffee in one of the Galata cafés.

    • Blue Mosque
    • Hagia Sophia
    • Basilica Cistern
    • Topkapı Palace
    • Grand Bazaar
    • Galata Tower
  3. Day3

    The Bosphorus, Maiden's Tower and Çamlıca

    We start at Dolmabahçe Palace, then Ortaköy and Bebek, and take a boat on a Bosphorus cruise between the palaces and fortresses. In the afternoon we cross to Üsküdar and take a short boat ride to Maiden's Tower in the middle of the water, then climb Çamlıca Hill and its mosque to watch the sunset over the city. The evening is free for dinner.

    • Dolmabahçe Palace
    • Ortaköy
    • Bebek
    • Bosphorus boat tour
    • Maiden's Tower
    • Çamlıca Hill and its mosque
  4. Day4

    Flight to Cappadocia and Red Valley sunset

    A domestic flight from Istanbul to Nevşehir Airport (NAV) or Kayseri (ASR) takes about an hour and a quarter, followed by about an hour by car to the cave hotel. In the afternoon we visit Uçhisar Castle and Pigeon Valley, and watch the sunset from the Red and Rose Valley viewpoint. The evening is at the hotel or at a restaurant set in the caves.

    • Nevşehir or Kayseri Airport
    • Cave hotel
    • Uçhisar Castle
    • Pigeon Valley
    • Red and Rose Valley viewpoint
  5. Day5

    Sunrise balloon and the Red Tour

    Before dawn, an optional balloon flight of about an hour over the valleys, or watch the balloons from the hotel terrace. After breakfast we visit Göreme Open-Air Museum with its rock-cut churches, then Paşabağ Valley and Devrent (Imagination) Valley, and finish in Avanos at a pottery workshop on the riverbank. In the evening you can attend the Turkish Night.

    • Balloon flight (optional)
    • Göreme Open-Air Museum
    • Paşabağ Valley
    • Imagination Valley (Devrent)
    • Avanos and pottery workshops
  6. Day6

    Love Valley, then on to Sapanca

    In the morning, a photo session in Love Valley and at the Göreme viewpoint, or an optional quad-bike ride. Then we head to the airport for the domestic flight to Sabiha Gökçen Airport in Istanbul, where the driver meets you and takes you to Sapanca in about an hour and a half. You arrive at the bungalow in the evening to rest.

    • Love Valley
    • Göreme viewpoint
    • Nevşehir or Kayseri Airport
    • Sabiha Gökçen Airport (SAW)
    • Bungalow in Sapanca
  7. Day7

    Maşukiye and Lake Sapanca

    A quiet morning at the bungalow, then we visit Maşukiye village with its waterfalls and restaurants among the trees, and ride the Kartepe cable car to see the forests from above. In the afternoon, a walk along Lake Sapanca and a boat ride at sunset, then a private evening at the bungalow with the jacuzzi. If you want some adventure, you can try horse riding or quad biking.

    • Maşukiye Village
    • Kartepe Cable Car
    • Sapanca Lake
    • Boat ride at sunset
  8. Day8

    Return to Istanbul and shopping

    We leave Sapanca in the morning for Istanbul, about two hours away. After check-in there is time to shop at Istinye Park, Nişantaşı Street or the Grand Bazaar for gifts. In the evening we suggest a farewell dinner on the Bosphorus or at one of the Bebek restaurants overlooking the water.

    • The road to Istanbul
    • Istinye Park or Nişantaşı
    • Farewell dinner on the Bosphorus (optional)
  9. Day9

    Transfer to the airport

    A free morning, depending on your flight time. The driver picks you up at the hotel and takes you to the airport about three hours before departure. We wish you a happy life together and a safe journey home.

    • Hotel check-out
    • Transfer to the airport

Questions & answers

Questions about the Turkey Honeymoon Program, 9 Days

What are the best places in Turkey for a honeymoon?

Istanbul for the romantic atmosphere on the Bosphorus, Cappadocia for cave hotels and balloons, Sapanca and Uzungöl for quiet bungalows in nature, and Antalya and Bodrum if you prefer the sea. This program combines the first three.

Is the Cappadocia balloon flight guaranteed?

It cannot be guaranteed: each morning the flight is decided based on the wind and clearance from the authorities. If the flight is canceled, we try to move it to the next morning if seats are available, or the amount is refunded according to the balloon company's policy.

Can you arrange a dinner on a private yacht on the Bosphorus?

Yes, you can add a private yacht dinner of two hours or more, or a dinner cruise with entertainment. The price varies with the size of the yacht, the duration and the season, and we include it in your quote.

Can Cappadocia be replaced with Turkey's Black Sea region?

Yes, days 4 to 6 can be spent in Uzungöl and Trabzon instead of Cappadocia, with the domestic flight to Trabzon Airport, and the rest of the program stays the same.
