Quick guide

Istanbul Airport or Sabiha Gökçen?

Istanbul has two major international airports, and choosing the right one saves you travel time and cost.

If your hotel is in Taksim or Sultanahmet, Istanbul Airport is usually the better fit; if you will stay on the Asian side or head to Sapanca, Sabiha Gökçen may be closer. We arrange your airport pickup from either airport.
Istanbul Airport (IST)Sabiha Gökçen Airport (SAW)
LocationEuropean side (north of the city)Asian side (southeast of the city)
AirlinesTurkish Airlines and most international airlinesPegasus, AJet and budget airlines
Best forTaksim, Şişli, Sultanahmet, the BosphorusThe Asian side, Sapanca, Bursa by road

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about booking flights

When should I book to get the best flight price?

As a rule, booking several weeks ahead gives you more options and better prices, especially in summer, holiday seasons and school breaks. Send us your dates and we will compare the available options across several airlines.

What is the difference between Istanbul Airport and Sabiha Gökçen Airport?

Istanbul Airport (IST) is on the European side and serves most international flights and Turkish Airlines, while Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) is on the Asian side and is used by low-cost airlines such as Pegasus. The airport you choose affects how long it takes to reach your hotel, and we guide you to the best fit.

What is the baggage allowance?

The baggage allowance varies by airline and ticket class; some economy fares do not include checked baggage. We confirm the allowance in writing before issuing the ticket, and extra weight can be added when needed.

Can the ticket be changed or canceled after booking?

It depends on the terms of the ticket you choose; some fares are flexible and allow changes for a fee, while others are non-refundable. We explain the terms before you pay, and we handle the change procedures for you if needed.

Do you provide a flight reservation for a visa application?

Yes, we can prepare a provisional confirmed flight reservation for your visa file, as required by the embassy or visa center, and then issue the final ticket once the visa is granted.

How long is the flight from Istanbul to Trabzon?

About 1 hour 45 minutes on direct flights, and several Turkish airlines operate daily flights between Istanbul and Trabzon, making it the fastest way to reach Turkey's Black Sea region.
