Day Trips from Istanbul: A Guide to Sapanca, Bursa and the Best Nearby Destinations

A practical guide to the most popular day trips from Istanbul: Sapanca and Maşukiye, Bursa and Uludağ, the Princes' Islands, and Şile and Ağva, with a comparison table and tips for choosing between a day trip and an overnight stay.

Istanbul is a city whose sights never run out, yet many visitors say the best days of their trip were spent outside it: by a lake, in a forest, or on a snow-covered mountain. In this guide we present the most popular day trips from Istanbul, led by Sapanca and Bursa, with approximate distances and the best seasons, and we help you choose what suits your family or your travel companions.

The distances and times given are approximate and are strongly affected by Istanbul's road traffic, especially on weekday mornings and weekends.

Sapanca and Maşukiye day trip from Istanbul

Sapanca (also spelled Sabanja) is a quiet town on the shores of a wide lake east of Istanbul, surrounded by hills and forests. It is the first choice for families looking for fresh air and greenery about two hours from the city.

The usual trip goes like this:

  1. Set out in the morning from your hotel by private car.
  2. Stop on the shores of Lake Sapanca for a walk and photos, with the option of a boat ride.
  3. Head to the village of Maşukiye and its waterfalls, and have lunch at riverside restaurants known for trout and grills.
  4. Ride the Kartepe cable car for mountain views, and visit one of the glass viewing platforms.
  5. Optional activities at extra cost: ATV safari, zip-lining and more.
  6. Return to Istanbul in the evening.

Please note that activities and tickets are usually not included in the tour price, and that some have age and weight restrictions. Read more in our Sapanca tourism guide.

Bursa and Mount Uludağ day trip

Bursa was the first capital of the Ottoman state, and it combines history, nature and mountain. The trip usually starts by crossing the Gulf of İzmit by ferry from Eskihisar, a short sea crossing of about half an hour, or by the highway over the Osmangazi Bridge.

Highlights of the day:

  • Uludağ cable car: A long line that takes you up to about 1800 meters, where there is snow in winter and refreshing air in summer.
  • The Grand Mosque (Ulu Cami): One of the best-known early Ottoman mosques, with its many domes.
  • The Green Mosque and Tomb: A beautiful example of green tile decoration.
  • Koza Han and the covered market: For buying silk, cotton towels and sweets.
  • The ancient plane tree in İnkaya: For a traditional tea break in its shade.
  • Cumalıkızık village: An Ottoman village of stone and timber houses, part of a site on the World Heritage List.

In winter Uludağ becomes a ski destination, and the season usually runs from December to March depending on snowfall. Full details on our Bursa tourism page.

Other trips worth trying

Princes' Islands

A group of islands in the Sea of Marmara, the largest of which is Büyükada. Private cars do not run there, and visitors get around on foot, by bicycle or by electric cart among the old wooden houses. It suits a quiet day away from the bustle of the city.

Şile and Ağva

On the Black Sea coast north of the Asian side: Şile Castle and its lighthouse, and the village of Ağva, set between two rivers, with the option of a boat trip on the river and lunch on its banks. It is ideal in summer.

Yalova and Bolu

Yalova is known for its thermal baths and waterfalls, while Lake Abant and the Seven Lakes park in Bolu are among the loveliest sights in autumn and in the snow, but they need a very long day, and it is better to stay overnight nearby.

A day trip or an overnight stay?

A one-day trip suits you if your stay in Istanbul is short, or if you prefer a single hotel for the whole trip. But staying overnight has clear advantages:

  • In Sapanca there are many bungalows and wooden cabins, some with a private pool or jacuzzi, an experience couples and families love.
  • In Bursa, staying overnight lets you go up Uludağ in the morning before the crowds, and visit the markets in the evening without rushing.
  • Fewer hours on the road in a single day, which matters with children and older travelers.

If you want to combine the two cities in one program with overnight stays, see our Istanbul, Sapanca and Bursa 8-day program, or ask us to recommend a suitable hotel or bungalow for your family.

Which trip should you choose for your kind of journey?

For families with children

Sapanca and Maşukiye are the easiest choice: a relatively comfortable road, green spaces to play in, animals and small farms along the way, and light activities suited to different ages. If your children love snow, set aside a day for Uludağ in winter, with waterproof clothing and extra gloves.

For couples and honeymooners

The Princes' Islands give you a quiet, romantic day between the sea and wooden houses, and Sapanca with a night in a bungalow overlooking nature is an experience many newlyweds choose. Ağva suits those who love rivers, boats and countryside restaurants.

For groups of friends

Bursa and Uludağ combine adventure and shopping, and Sapanca offers plenty of thrilling activities such as ATVs and zip-lining. Larger groups can choose a private minibus to reduce the cost per person.

For older travelers

Choose a shorter trip at a relaxed pace, such as a lake tour in Sapanca with lunch by the river, and avoid very long days such as Bolu. Tell us in advance about any special needs so we can choose a comfortable car and suitable stops.

Tips for a successful day trip

  1. Set out early: Leaving Istanbul before the morning peak saves you an hour or more.
  2. Avoid weekends if you can: Locals head to the same destinations, and restaurants and cable cars get crowded.
  3. Dress in layers: It is clearly colder at the top of Uludağ or in Maşukiye than in Istanbul, even in summer.
  4. Carry cash in lira: Some small kiosks and village activities do not accept cards.
  5. Ask what is included before you book: Is the price per car or per person? Does it include the ferry, fuel and bridge tolls? Which activities are not included?
  6. Group trip or private car? Group trips are cheaper for individuals, while a private car suits families better because it gives you freedom with timing and stops.

Day trips in winter

From December to March the Bursa and Uludağ trip is the star of the season thanks to the snow, while Sapanca and Kartepe take on a quiet winter atmosphere. If your trip is in winter, the Istanbul and Uludağ winter program combines the city and the snow in a comfortable balance.

What to bring on a day trip

  • An extra jacket or a light coat, and a small umbrella in autumn and winter.
  • Comfortable shoes suitable for walking on lakeside paths and mountain roads.
  • Water and snacks for children, as some distances between stops are long.
  • A portable phone charger, because the photo-taking never stops in Sapanca and Uludağ.
  • Your passport or a copy of it, as you may need it at some checkpoints or activities.

Questions & answers

Related questions

How far is Sapanca from Istanbul?

Sapanca lies about 140 kilometers from Istanbul, roughly two hours by car depending on traffic, and a full day trip including Maşukiye takes about 9 to 10 hours.

Is one day enough to visit Bursa?

It is enough to see the main sights and ride the Uludağ cable car, but it makes for a long day. If you want to ski, or to visit Cumalıkızık village and the markets at a relaxed pace, an overnight stay in Bursa is better.

When is there snow on Mount Uludağ?

The snow and ski season on Uludağ usually runs from December to March and varies from year to year depending on snowfall.

Are activities such as ATVs included in the tour price?

Usually not. Activities, cable car tickets and restaurants are paid separately on most tours. We explain what the price includes before you book.
