Day trips from Istanbul and Trabzon: 10 selected trips

At a glance

Day trips are the easiest way to see more than one face of Turkey while staying in a single hotel. From Istanbul you can reach Bursa and Mount Uludağ in one day, or Lake Sapanca and the forests of Maşukiye, or stay in the city among the Princes' Islands, the Bosphorus and the old city. Every tour starts at your hotel door and ends there, so you don't need to carry bags or change where you stay.

And from Trabzon you set off to Lake Uzungöl, or to the Ayder Plateau and the tea plantations of Rize, or to Sümela Monastery hanging on the mountain. We arrange every tour in a private car for you and your family, so you control the pace, the number of stops, and prayer and meal times. In the north in particular, distances between valleys, lakes and plateaus are long, and a driver who knows the mountain roads makes a clear difference to your day.

The durations given are approximate and change with traffic, weather and how long you stay at each stop. Entrance tickets, cable cars and optional activities are paid separately unless stated in the quote, and we tell you about them in advance as far as possible. If your trip is in winter, we suggest the tours best suited to the weather and road conditions.

What the service includes

  • A private car with an English-speaking driver.
  • Pickup from your hotel in Istanbul or Trabzon and return to it.
  • Fuel within the agreed tour route.
  • Stops arranged to suit you, including prayer and meal times.
  • A licensed guide or extra activities arranged on request.

Bursa and Mount Uludağ

You take the cable car up Uludağ, where there is snow in winter and forests in summer, then come down to old Bursa with its early Ottoman mosques and bazaars. We recommend setting off early, because the drive takes about two to three hours each way depending on traffic.

Questions & answers

FAQ

What is the best day trip from Istanbul for families?

Sapanca and Maşukiye are a comfortable choice for most families thanks to the nature and the riverside restaurants, and Bursa and Uludağ suit anyone who wants snow in winter. For a shorter drive, the Princes' Islands or Polonezköy are a calmer choice.

Is a one-day trip enough to visit Sapanca?

Yes, one day is enough to see the lake, Maşukiye and lunch by the river. If you want to stay overnight among the forests, we recommend a program with two nights there, such as the 8-day Istanbul, Sapanca and Bursa program.

Can I visit Uzungöl in one day from Trabzon?

Yes, the drive is about an hour and a half to two hours each way, and the full tour takes about 10 hours. If you want to enjoy the lake in the morning and evening, it is better to stay one or two nights there.

Are entrance fees and the cable car included in the day trips?

Usually not: the tour includes the car and driver, while museum tickets, cable cars, boats and activities are paid separately. We explain in the quote what is included and what is not.

Are the day trips private or group tours?

We arrange the tours in a private car for you and your family or group only, with an English-speaking driver. This gives you freedom with timing and the number of stops, instead of sticking to a group bus program.

When is there snow on Uludağ?

Snow usually covers Uludağ from about December to March, and this varies from season to season. We check the road and cable car conditions before the day of the trip.
