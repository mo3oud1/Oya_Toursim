Maldives honeymoon: 5-day program at a private-island resort

Four nights at a resort on a private island, with an overwater villa, coral reefs and sunset boat trips.

Malé → private-island resortHoneymoonLuxuryBeaches
Book this programThe program day by day

About the program

A Maldives honeymoon is an experience like no other: a small island occupied only by your resort, clear turquoise water beneath your villa, and coral reefs steps from the beach. We designed the 5-day Maldives tour to be a truly relaxing getaway, with a transfer from the airport by speedboat or seaplane depending on the resort's location. The Maldives is made up of more than a thousand coral islands in the Indian Ocean, and many of its resorts occupy an entire island of their own.

Direct flights from Istanbul to Malé are available, and the program is built around a single resort that we choose with you according to your budget and style: a beach villa for families, or an overwater villa with a private pool for newlyweds, with a half-board, full-board or all-inclusive meal plan. The stay can easily be split between two resorts or extended. We help you compare resorts clearly by location, how you get there and what the meal plan includes.

Trip highlights

  • An overwater or beach villa at a private-island resort
  • Speedboat or seaplane transfer over the coral islands
  • Snorkeling among the coral reefs and colorful fish
  • Sunset cruise with a chance to see dolphins
  • A stroll on a secluded sandbank in the middle of the ocean
  • Romantic dinner on the beach (optional)

The program day by day

  1. Day1

    Arrive in Malé and transfer to the resort

    Pick-up at Velana International Airport by a resort representative, then transfer to the island by speedboat if the resort is close, or by seaplane on a panoramic flight over the coral islands. Check in at the villa, and the rest of the day is for relaxing and getting to know the resort.

    • Velana International Airport
    • Speedboat or seaplane
    • Resort
  2. Day2

    Coral reef day

    A day devoted to the sea: snorkeling on the coral reefs around the island, with colorful fish and sometimes turtles and harmless small sharks. In the afternoon there is time for the spa, or for swimming straight from your villa's terrace.

    • The coral reefs around the island
    • Diving center
    • Spa
  3. Day3

    Sandbank and sunset cruise

    A boat trip to a secluded white sandbank in the middle of the ocean for hours of calm, swimming and photos. At sunset, a cruise with a good chance of seeing the dolphins that are often spotted in Maldivian waters.

    • Sandbank
    • Sunset dolphin cruise
  4. Day4

    Free day and optional activities

    A completely free day to choose what suits you: kayaking or paddleboarding, a scuba diving trip for beginners and experienced divers, or a private dinner on the beach. At some resorts you can book a trip to swim with manta rays or whale sharks, depending on season and location.

    • Water sports
    • Beach dinner
    • Manta ray trip (seasonal)
  5. Day5

    Departure

    A last breakfast by the sea, then check-out and transfer to Velana Airport. If time allows, you can take a short tour of the capital, Malé, including the old Friday Mosque built from coral stone, before your return flight to Istanbul.

    • Resort
    • Malé
    • Hukuru Miskiy (Old Friday Mosque)
    • Velana International Airport

Included in the program

  • Pick-up at Malé Airport by a resort representative
  • Round-trip transfer by speedboat or seaplane, depending on the resort
  • 4 nights at the chosen resort on the agreed meal plan (half board, full board or all-inclusive)
  • Sandbank excursion and sunset cruise (according to the resort's program)
  • Follow-up and support on WhatsApp throughout the trip

Not included

  • Flights from Istanbul (can be added on request)
  • The green tax and resort fees, if not stated in the quote
  • Scuba diving trips and optional activities
  • Drinks and meals not covered by the meal plan
  • Travel insurance and personal expenses

Questions & answers

Questions about the Maldives 5-day tour

How much does the Maldives cost for a week?

The cost varies a great deal depending on the resort, the villa type, the meal plan, the transfer method and the season. Send us your budget and dates on WhatsApp, and we will suggest suitable resorts with a detailed quote.

What is the best island or resort in the Maldives?

There is no single best resort for everyone. One close to Malé shortens transfer time, one with a nearby house reef suits snorkeling lovers, and resorts with kids' clubs suit families. Tell us your priorities and we will recommend the best fit.

What is the difference between a speedboat and a seaplane transfer?

The speedboat serves resorts close to Malé and runs day and night, while the seaplane serves distant resorts and gives you a wonderful view of the islands, but it runs in daylight only and costs more.

Is the Maldives also suitable for families?

Yes, many resorts offer spacious beach villas, kids' clubs and relatively safe shallow beaches. We choose a resort that suits your children's ages.
