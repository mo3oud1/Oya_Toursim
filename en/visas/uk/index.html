Overview

Turkish citizens and most non-Gulf Arab nationalities need a Standard Visitor visa to enter the UK, and the ETA electronic travel authorisation does not cover Turkish passport holders. The visa allows a stay of up to 6 months per visit for tourism, visiting family or short business trips.

The steps begin with filling in the application on the GOV.UK website and paying the fee, then attending a biometrics appointment at one of the TLScontact centers in Istanbul, Ankara or Izmir according to the latest announcements. A decision is usually issued about 3 weeks after the biometrics, with paid priority services for those who need a faster decision.

The strength of the file lies in the details here: six months of bank statements, proof of employment, business activity or study, and a clear travel plan. Any document not in English needs a certified translation, and we arrange the translations and review the consistency of the file before you apply. The decision ultimately rests with the UK Home Office.

Who can apply?

  • Turkish citizens: they need a visit visa, and the ETA system does not cover them.
  • Residents of Turkey who are nationals of Arab countries, with the Turkish residence permit included in the file.
  • Jordanians: they have been back on the list of those who need a visa since September 2024.
  • Citizens of the Gulf countries (Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman): an ETA electronic travel authorisation is enough instead of a visa.
  • Frequent travelers: they can apply for a long-term visit visa valid for 2, 5 or 10 years.

Types and options

Types of UK visa

Standard Visitor visa (6 months)

For tourism, visiting family and friends, and short business trips, with a stay of up to 6 months.

Long-term visit visa

Valid for 2, 5 or 10 years for those who travel to the UK regularly, with the stay limit remaining 6 months per visit, and a higher fee.

ETA electronic travel authorisation for Gulf citizens

Valid for two years or until the passport expires, for multiple trips. A decision is usually issued within a day and may take up to 3 working days.

Priority services

Paid services to speed up the decision where available, without affecting the outcome of the application.

Checklist

Required papers and documents

The general list is below, and requirements may vary by nationality and country of residence — we confirm them for you before you apply.

  • Your current passport and old passports.
  • Bank statements for the last 6 months.
  • Proof of employment, business activity or study (employer letter, SGK record, trade registry, student certificate).
  • Accommodation details and travel itinerary.
  • An invitation letter if you are visiting someone in the UK.
  • For non-Turkish residents: the Turkish residence permit card (ikamet).
  • A certified English translation of every document that is not in English.

Questions & answers

UK visa FAQ

How long does a UK visa from Turkey take?

A decision is usually issued about 3 weeks after the biometrics appointment, and paid priority services are available to speed it up. We recommend applying well before you travel.

Where do I give biometrics for a UK visa in Istanbul?

At a TLScontact center, and there are also centers in Ankara and Izmir according to the latest announcements. We help you book a suitable appointment after you complete the online application.

How much is the UK visa fee?

The official fee is set by the UK government. It varies by visa type and duration and may change, so we confirm it with you at the time of application. We help you fill in the application, organize your file and book your biometrics appointment for a small service fee that we explain to you before we start.

Does a Gulf citizen need a UK visa?

Citizens of the Gulf countries do not need a visit visa. They need an ETA electronic travel authorisation, valid for two years or until the passport expires.

Do Turkish documents have to be translated?

Yes. Every document that is not in English needs a certified English translation, and we arrange the translation as part of our service.

Can a resident of Turkey apply for a UK visa?

Yes. A resident with a valid residence permit in Turkey applies and attaches the permit to the file, along with proof of employment, income and ties to Turkey. The decision rests with the UK Home Office.
