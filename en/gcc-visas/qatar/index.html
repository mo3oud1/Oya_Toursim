Qatar Visa from Turkey: Exemption, the Hayya Platform and the Tourist Visa

You may not need a Qatar visa at all: many nationalities, including Turkish citizens, enter Qatar without a prior visa, while others apply through the Hayya platform. At OYA Tourism we work out the right route for you and prepare your application in full.

Overview

Qatar exempts citizens of more than a hundred countries from an advance visa, according to the official Visit Qatar platform, and Turkey is among them under a mutual exemption for holders of ordinary passports. Citizens of the Gulf Cooperation Council countries also enter without a visa. The permitted length of stay varies by nationality, and we confirm it with you before you travel.

Those not covered by the exemption list, including many Syrians, Iraqis, Egyptians and Yemenis living in Turkey, apply through the Hayya platform for Qatar. The platform includes the A1 tourist visa, the A2 visa for residents of the Gulf countries, the A3 Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) for holders of a valid visa or residence permit from Schengen countries, the UK, the US, Canada, Australia or New Zealand, and the A4 visa for companions of citizens of the Council countries.

The application usually requires a confirmed accommodation booking or host details, a return ticket, and a passport valid for at least 3 months on arrival. We fill in the application on the platform, book your hotel and flights, and review every detail before submission. The approval decision rests with the Qatari authorities.

Who can apply?

  • Turkish citizens: exempt from a visa under a mutual agreement, and we help them with bookings and insurance.
  • Residents of Turkey of non-exempt nationalities: they usually apply for the A1 tourist visa through the Hayya platform.
  • Holders of a valid residence permit or visa from the UK, the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand or Schengen countries: they may be eligible for the A3 Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA).
  • Residents of the Gulf Cooperation Council countries with a valid residence permit: category A2.
  • Companions of citizens of the Council countries: category A4.

Types and options

Types of Qatar visa

Visa-free entry

For citizens of the countries covered by the exemption, including Turkey. A valid passport with a return ticket and accommodation booking is enough, and the length of stay is set according to nationality.

Hayya A1 tourist visa

For nationalities that are not exempt and not eligible for the ETA. It requires an accommodation booking or a host, and some nationalities may be required to book their hotel through the Discover Qatar platform.

A2 visa for Gulf residents

For residents of the Gulf Cooperation Council countries with a valid residence permit.

A4 visa for companions of Gulf citizens

For those accompanying a Gulf citizen on a trip to Qatar.

A3 Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA)

For holders of a valid residence permit or visa from the UK, the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand or Schengen countries.

Questions & answers

Qatar visa FAQ

Does a Turkish citizen need a Qatar visa?

No. Holders of an ordinary Turkish passport enjoy a mutual visa exemption. A valid passport with a return ticket and accommodation booking is enough, and we check the permitted length of stay for you before you travel.

What is the Hayya platform for Qatar?

Hayya is the official platform for visit visa applications to Qatar, and it includes categories A1, A2, A3 (Electronic Travel Authorization, ETA) and A4. We fill in your application on it and follow it up.

How much does the Qatar tourist visa cost?

The Visit Qatar platform sets the official fees, and they may change, so we confirm them with you at the time of application. We submit the application for you and prepare your file correctly for a small service fee that we explain to you before we start.

I live in Turkey and hold a Schengen visa. Do I need an A1 visa?

If you have a valid visa or residence permit from Schengen countries, the UK, the US or other eligible countries, you may be eligible for the A3 Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) instead of A1. Send us a photo of your passport and visa via WhatsApp and we will check your eligibility.

Do I need health insurance for a Qatar visa?

We strongly recommend health insurance that covers your whole stay. We arrange it for you as part of your file, and we let you know if your visa category requires it at the time of application.

How long does it take to get a Qatar visa?

The time varies by category and nationality, from a few working days to several weeks at times, so we advise applying early.
