14-day Turkey tour program: Istanbul, Cappadocia, Antalya and Trabzon

Two weeks to see all of Turkey: Istanbul, the cave hotels of Cappadocia, the historic Konya road, Antalya's beaches, then Trabzon and Uzungöl.

About the program

The 14-day Turkey tour program is the grand tour for anyone who wants to see the different faces of Turkey in a single trip: Istanbul with its history and markets, Cappadocia with its valleys and balloons, Konya, the city of Mevlana Jalaluddin Rumi, Antalya with its turquoise beaches, and then the green north in Trabzon and Lake Uzungöl. Each region has its own character, weather and cuisine, and that is what makes the trip so rich.

We planned the route so the travel makes sense: a domestic flight from Istanbul to Cappadocia, then a road trip via Konya to Antalya that becomes a tour in its own right, then a domestic flight to Trabzon, and finally a flight back to Istanbul for shopping before departure. Domestic flights are included, and in every city you have a private car with a driver. The program suits families and friends, and a destination can be dropped or another extended to fit your vacation. We send you the final offer with the available domestic flight times.

Trip highlights

  • 5 nights in Istanbul: the old city, the Bosphorus and a shopping day
  • 2 nights in a Cappadocia cave hotel, with a balloon option
  • A historic road route via Sultanhanı, Konya and the Mevlana Museum
  • 3 nights in Antalya: Kaleiçi, the Düden Waterfalls and Kemer
  • Trabzon, Sümela Monastery and a night on Lake Uzungöl
  • 4 domestic flights included to save time
  • A private driver in every city and pickup at every airport

The program day by day

  1. Day1

    Arrival in Istanbul

    Your driver meets you at Istanbul Airport (IST) or Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) and takes you to the hotel. After a rest, enjoy a light evening walk around Taksim Square and İstiklal Avenue. We hand you a SIM card and go over the details of your domestic flights.

    • Istanbul Airport or Sabiha Gökçen
    • Hotel check-in
    • Taksim and İstiklal Avenue
  2. Day2

    The Old City and the Bazaars

    We visit the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia and the Basilica Cistern, then Topkapı Palace, which overlooks the meeting point of the Bosphorus and the Sea of Marmara. In the afternoon we see the Süleymaniye Mosque, then the Grand Bazaar and the Spice Bazaar in Eminönü. We end the day on the Galata Bridge at sunset.

    • Blue Mosque
    • Hagia Sophia
    • Basilica Cistern
    • Topkapı Palace
    • Süleymaniye Mosque
    • Grand Bazaar
    • Spice Bazaar
  3. Day3

    The Bosphorus and Çamlıca

    We start at Dolmabahçe Palace, then Ortaköy, Bebek and Rumeli Hisarı Fortress, and take a boat tour of the Bosphorus of about an hour and a half. Then we cross to the Asian side to visit the Çamlıca Mosque and watch the sunset from its hill. An optional seafood dinner can be added.

    • Dolmabahçe Palace
    • Ortaköy
    • Bebek
    • Rumeli Fortress
    • Bosphorus boat tour
    • Çamlıca Mosque and Hill
  4. Day4

    Flight to Cappadocia

    A morning domestic flight to Nevşehir Airport (NAV) or Kayseri (ASR), about an hour and a quarter, then roughly an hour by car to the cave hotel. In the afternoon we visit Uçhisar Castle and Pigeon Valley, and watch the sunset from a viewpoint over the Red and Rose Valleys. The evening is free at the hotel or in one of the cave restaurants.

    • Nevşehir or Kayseri Airport
    • Cave hotel
    • Uçhisar Castle
    • Pigeon Valley
    • Red and Rose Valley viewpoint
  5. Day5

    Balloons, Cappadocia valleys and the underground city

    Before dawn, take the optional balloon flight or watch it from the viewpoints. After breakfast we visit the Göreme Open-Air Museum, Paşabağ Valley and Devrent Valley, then the pottery workshops in Avanos. In the afternoon we visit the underground city of Kaymaklı or Derinkuyu, with its floors carved into the rock.

    • Balloon flight (optional)
    • Göreme Open-Air Museum
    • Paşabağ Valley
    • Imagination Valley (Devrent)
    • Avanos
    • Kaymaklı or Derinkuyu underground city
  6. Day6

    The Silk Road: Sultanhanı, Konya, then Antalya

    A long road day (about 7 hours of driving) that we break up with memorable stops. First we stop at Sultanhanı, one of the largest Seljuk caravanserais on the caravan route. Then we reach Konya to visit the Mevlana Museum, home to the tomb of Jalaluddin Rumi, and have lunch. In the afternoon we cross the Taurus Mountains down to Antalya and reach the hotel in the evening.

    • Sultanhanı Caravanserai (Sultan Han)
    • Konya
    • Mevlana Jalaluddin Rumi Museum
    • Taurus Mountains
    • Hotel in Antalya
  7. Day7

    Antalya: Kaleiçi and the Düden Waterfalls

    We begin at the Roman Hadrian's Gate and the old lanes of Kaleiçi down to the old harbor, then take a boat trip to see the Lower Düden Waterfall plunge into the sea (in season). In the afternoon we visit the Upper Düden Waterfalls, then watch the sunset at Konyaaltı Beach. The evening is left free in the lanes and restaurants of Kaleiçi.

    • Hadrian's Gate
    • Kaleiçi district
    • The old harbor
    • Boat trip to the Lower Düden Waterfall
    • Upper Düden Waterfalls
    • Konyaaltı Beach
  8. Day8

    Kemer and the Olympos Cable Car, or a beach day

    We head to Kemer and ride the Olympos cable car to the top of Mount Tahtalı for views over the coast, then swim or stroll in Moonlight Park and on its beach. If you prefer to rest, you can spend the whole day at the hotel beach or pool. We recommend packing your bags in the evening for tomorrow's trip to Trabzon.

    • Olympos cable car (Tahtalı Mountain)
    • Moonlight Park in Kemer
    • Kemer Marina
    • Alternative: a free day at the beach
  9. Day9

    Flight to Trabzon

    A domestic flight from Antalya to Trabzon, direct if available in season or via Istanbul, set according to the schedule at the time of booking. Our driver meets you at Trabzon Airport, and if time allows we visit Trabzon's Hagia Sophia and, before sunset, go up Boztepe Hill overlooking the Black Sea. The evening is free in Meydan Square, near the hotel.

    • Antalya Airport (AYT)
    • Trabzon Airport (TZX)
    • Hagia Sophia of Trabzon
    • Boztepe Hill
  10. Day10

    Sümela Monastery, Hamsiköy and Lake Sera

    We drive to the Altındere Valley in about an hour to visit Sümela Monastery, perched on the cliff; reaching it takes a walk uphill of a few minutes. Then we go up to the village of Hamsiköy to taste rice pudding with hazelnuts. On the way back we stop at Lake Sera, west of the city, for a stroll, then enjoy a free evening in Meydan Square.

    • Altındere Valley
    • Sümela Monastery
    • Hamsiköy village
    • Lake Sera
    • Meydan Square
  11. Day11

    To Lake Uzungöl

    We set off for Uzungöl, about two hours with stops, at the Sürmene knife shops, a tea house and waterfalls along the way. After check-in we walk around the lake and go up to the Uzungöl panoramic viewpoint, visiting a horse farm and honey shops. A quiet night by the lake.

    • Sürmene
    • Tea shop or factory
    • Lake Uzungöl
    • Panoramic Uzungöl viewpoint
    • Horse farm
  12. Day12

    Uzungöl morning and back to Istanbul

    A quiet morning by the lake before it fills with visitors, then we drive to Trabzon Airport, about two hours, for the domestic flight to Istanbul (about two hours). The driver meets you in Istanbul and takes you to the hotel. The evening is free to rest after a day of travel.

    • Lake Uzungöl in the morning
    • Trabzon Airport (TZX)
    • Istanbul Airport or Sabiha Gökçen
    • Hotel in Istanbul
  13. Day13

    Shopping day in Istanbul

    A free day with the driver for shopping at one of the big malls or the clothing markets in Laleli and Osmanbey, and for gifts from the Grand Bazaar. Alternatively, you can visit the Princes' Islands by ferry. We suggest a farewell dinner on the Bosphorus.

    • Mall of Istanbul or Istinye Park
    • Laleli and Osmanbey markets
    • Alternative: the Princes' Islands
  14. Day14

    Transfer to the airport

    A free morning, depending on your flight time. The driver picks you up at the hotel and takes you to the airport about three hours before departure. We wish you a safe journey home.

    • Hotel check-out
    • Transfer to the airport

Questions & answers

Questions about the Grand Turkey program, 14 days

Is 14 days enough to see Turkey?

It is enough to see the four main tourist regions without rushing: Istanbul, Cappadocia, Antalya and Turkey's Black Sea region. If you want more time by the sea, you can add nights in Antalya, and if you prefer nature, add nights in Uzungöl.

Why go by road from Cappadocia to Antalya?

A suitable direct flight may not be available on your travel date, and flying via Istanbul takes up most of the day. The road route via Konya (about 7 hours of driving) turns the transfer day into a tour of Sultanhanı and the Mevlana Museum, and it can be replaced by a flight.

Can I drop a destination or shorten the program?

Yes. For example, you can drop Antalya to make it a 10-day trip close to the 10-day Istanbul and Trabzon program, or drop the Black Sea region and keep only Istanbul, Cappadocia and Antalya.

Is the program suitable for families with children?

Yes, though note that the road day to Antalya is long and can be replaced by a flight. We provide family rooms, a spacious van and child seats on request.
