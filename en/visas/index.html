UK Visa

US Visa

The US visa from Turkey (B1/B2 visitor visa) requires the DS-160 form and an in-person interview at the US Embassy in Ankara or the US Consulate General in Istanbul. At OYA Tourism we prepare your application and file, book your interview appointment and prepare you for it, with honesty and clarity from the start.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about international visas

Can someone living in Turkey apply for a Schengen visa from Istanbul?

Most often yes, if you hold a valid residence permit in Turkey that the visa center of the country concerned accepts. Requirements differ between countries and residence types, and we review your situation before we start.

What documents are required for a Schengen visa?

Usually: a valid passport, the application form, recent photos, proof of residence, a bank statement, proof of employment or study, hotel and flight reservations, and travel insurance. The details are on our Schengen visa page.

Do you book visa center appointments?

Official visa center appointments are free and are booked on their official websites. We guide you through the booking steps and prepare your file in full. We do not sell “guaranteed appointments”, and we advise against dealing with anyone who claims to.

Do you guarantee visa approval?

No. The decision rests with the embassy or consulate alone. Our role is to raise the quality of your file and avoid the common mistakes that cause rejection.

Do you help with easy e-visas?

Yes, such as visas for Azerbaijan, some destinations in Asia and others, depending on your nationality, and we link them to our tour programs outside Turkey.
