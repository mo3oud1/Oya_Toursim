Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

About Us

OYA Tourism is a travel agency based in Istanbul, Turkey. We are responsible for the data you send us, and you can contact us about it at the email or WhatsApp number listed at the bottom of the page.

The data we collect and why

When you fill in the website forms, the data is sent directly from your device to the WhatsApp app or to your email; the website itself does not store this data on its own servers.

When you book, we may ask for data such as your name, phone number, passport details, travel dates and number of travelers, only to carry out the service you requested.

Travel documents and passport photos

We ask for a photo of your passport or residence permit only when the service requires it (visa, flight ticket, hotel booking). Send your documents only to our official number: +90 539 933 43 31. We use them only for the request they were sent for.

We ask for the data of traveling children (name, age and passport details) from their guardian and only for the purpose of the booking.

Who we share your data with

We share your data only with the service providers needed to carry out your request (hotels, airlines, visa authorities, transport companies) and only to the extent necessary, and we do not sell it or share it for marketing purposes.

WhatsApp and email

We communicate with you via WhatsApp and email, which are services provided by other parties, and messages and attachments may be processed on servers outside Turkey according to their own privacy policies.

What is saved in your browser

To make filling in forms easier, the website saves a draft of the form in your browser on your device only; it is not sent to us and is deleted automatically after the request is sent. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings (clear site data).

Your rights

You can at any time ask to access, correct or delete your data by contacting us, in accordance with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and applicable regulations, unless the law requires us to retain it.

Cookies and analytics

The website currently does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools. If we add an analytics tool later, we will update this page first.

For inquiries: info@oyatourism.com or WhatsApp +90 539 933 43 31.
