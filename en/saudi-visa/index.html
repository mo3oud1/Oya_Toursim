An application can be rejected or its fees lost over a small mistake: a letter in the name that does not match the passport, a missing number, a date written the wrong way round, or a photo of the wrong size. Saudi Tourist Visa

The Saudi electronic tourist visa is the easiest way to visit the Kingdom for citizens of eligible countries, including Turkey, and for holders of used US, UK and Schengen visas. At OYA Tourism we check your eligibility, prepare your application and follow it up, and arrange your flight and hotel with it.

Entry type: multiple or single entryLength of stay: up to 90 days

Umrah Visa

We arrange an Umrah visa from Turkey for you from start to arrival: preparing the application through the Nusuk Umrah platform or authorized Umrah agents, booking your flight and hotels in Makkah and Madinah, then guiding you to book your Umrah permit.

Entry type: depends on the type: a regular Umrah visa or a multiple-entry annual oneLength of stay: up to 3 months after arrival

Family Visit Visa

The family visit visa lets you visit a relative who lives in Saudi Arabia, with an application submitted by the relative from inside the Kingdom. At OYA Tourism we prepare your file in Turkey, book your visa center appointment, and follow the application until the decision is issued.

Entry type: single or multiple, depending on the application and nationalityLength of stay: stated on the issued visa
Details and requirements

Personal Visit Visa

The Saudi personal visit visa is for visiting a friend or acquaintance in the Kingdom with no business purpose, by an invitation issued by the host through the visa platform. We help you prepare your file from Turkey and follow it up until the decision is issued.

Entry type: stated on the issued visaLength of stay: stated on the issued visa
Details and requirements

Business Visit Visa

The Saudi business visit visa is for attending meetings and conferences and exploring partnerships, by invitation from a Saudi company. We prepare your file from Turkey and follow it up, and arrange your flight and hotel in Riyadh or Jeddah with it.

Entry type: single or multipleLength of stay: up to 90 days per visit
Details and requirements

Transit Visa

If your trip from Istanbul to another destination connects through Saudi Arabia on Saudia or flynas, you can request a Saudi transit visa and stop in the Kingdom for up to 96 hours. We book your flight on the right route and help you apply for the visa.

Entry type: tied to the transit flightLength of stay: up to 96 hours
Details and requirements

Tourist Visa for GCC Residents

If you live in the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain or Oman, you may be able to get the Saudi tourist visa for Gulf residents based on your residence, even if your nationality is not on the list. We check your eligibility and prepare your application remotely via WhatsApp.

Visa type: electronic touristResidency requirement: a valid Gulf residence permit with at least 3 months remaining

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about Saudi visas

Which visa is right for me to visit Saudi Arabia?

It depends on the purpose of your visit and your nationality: for tourism, and for Umrah outside the Hajj season, it is often the tourist visa for those who meet its conditions; for Umrah there is the Umrah visa; for visiting relatives, the family visit; and for business, the business visit visa. Send us your details and we will find the most suitable one for you.

Can I perform Umrah on a tourist visa?

According to the published regulations, the tourist visa allows you to perform Umrah outside the Hajj season, with an Umrah appointment booked through the Nusuk app. We will confirm the latest updates for you before you travel.

Can someone living in Turkey get a Saudi visa?

It depends on their nationality and the type of visa: Turkish citizens are among the countries eligible for the electronic tourist visa, while other nationalities are subject to different conditions. Contact us with your nationality and the type of your residence permit and we will tell you your options.

How long does it take to issue the visa?

The time varies by visa type: electronic visas are usually issued quickly, while family visit and business visas take longer. We give you a realistic estimate when we open your application.

Do you guarantee visa approval?

No service provider can guarantee approval, as the decision rests with the Saudi authorities alone. Our role is to prepare a correct and complete file and follow it up, to improve the chances of acceptance and avoid mistakes.
