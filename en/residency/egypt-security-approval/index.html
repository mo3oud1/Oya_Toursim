Security approval for entering Egypt: application preparation and follow-up

Egypt requires travelers of some nationalities to obtain prior security approval before traveling there. We first check whether you need it in your case, then prepare the application and follow it up, and arrange your flight and hotel after it is issued.

At a glance

The list of nationalities that need security approval is set by the Egyptian authorities and changes, and the conditions may differ by age, country of residence and purpose of visit. That is why we start by checking your own case, not a general list.

Security approval is not a ticket or a booking. It is a prior authorization, and you may also need an entry visa depending on your nationality. We explain exactly what you need before you start.

What the service includes

  • Checking whether you need the approval in your case.
  • A clear list of the required documents.
  • Submitting the application and following it up until the decision is issued.
  • Booking flights, hotel and airport pickup after the approval is issued, if you wish.

Questions & answers

FAQ

Who needs security approval to enter Egypt?

Travelers of specific nationalities; the Egyptian authorities set the list and change it, and the conditions may differ depending on age and country of residence. Send us your nationality and details and we check for you.

How long does the security approval take?

The time varies from one application to another and cannot be stated with certainty. We tell you the expected time when you apply, and we advise you to start well ahead of your travel date.

Is the security approval the same thing as the visa?

No. The approval is a prior authorization, and some nationalities also need an entry visa alongside it. We explain what you need based on your nationality.

Do you guarantee that the approval will be issued?

No one can guarantee that; the decision rests with the Egyptian authorities alone. Our role is to submit a complete application and follow it up.
