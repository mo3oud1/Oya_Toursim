7 days • 6 nights • Georgia

7-day Georgia tour program: Tbilisi, Kazbegi and Borjomi

A week between Tbilisi's old alleys and the Caucasus peaks at Kazbegi and Gudauri, then the quiet forests and mineral waters of Borjomi.

Tbilisi → Kazbegi → Gudauri → BorjomiFamilyNatureFriends
Book this programThe program day by day

About the program

The 7-day Georgia tour program brings together the best of this country close to Istanbul: a lively capital, a historic mountain road that leads you to the peaks of the Caucasus, and a forest and mineral-water resort in Borjomi. The flight from Istanbul to Tbilisi takes only about two hours, so your vacation begins from day one without exhaustion. Georgia stands out for its striking variety in a small area: in a single day you go from the capital's streets to green valleys and snow-covered peaks, and the next day to pine forests and springs.

We designed the program to suit families and friends: tours by private car, comfortable transfers, and enough free time for shopping and relaxing. We spend a night in the mountains near Kazbegi and Gudauri so you can enjoy a calm Caucasus morning, then head west to Borjomi and Bakuriani. The program can be adjusted by adding Batumi on the Black Sea for anyone who wants to extend their stay. We choose comfortable hotels near the sights, and we help you arrange the travel insurance required for entry.

Trip highlights

  • A tour of old Tbilisi, Narikala Fortress by cable car and the Bridge of Peace
  • The Georgian Military Road, passing Ananuri Fortress and Zhinvali Reservoir
  • Gergeti Church with its view of Mount Kazbek in Kazbegi
  • The Friendship Monument in Gudauri and optional paragliding
  • The historic town of Mtskheta, a UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Borjomi Central Park and its famous mineral water spring
  • The Bakuriani cable car among pine forests

The program day by day

  1. Day1

    Arrival in Tbilisi

    Pickup at Tbilisi International Airport after a short flight from Istanbul, then we head to the hotel to check in and rest. In the evening we suggest an easy stroll along Rustaveli Avenue, the capital's main street, with its cafes and classical buildings. A quiet first night to get ready for days full of scenery.

    • Tbilisi International Airport
    • Hotel
    • Rustaveli Avenue
  2. Day2

    A tour of old Tbilisi

    We start at the sulfur baths district (Abanotubani) with its distinctive domes and pass the nearby Jumah Mosque of Tbilisi. Then we take the cable car from Rike Park up to Narikala Fortress for a panoramic view of the city and the Kura River. After that we cross the glass Bridge of Peace and wander the lanes of the old town, ending the day at Mtatsminda Park at the top of the city.

    • The sulfur baths district
    • Jumah Mosque of Tbilisi
    • Rike Park and the cable car
    • Narikala Fortress
    • Bridge of Peace
    • Mtatsminda Park
  3. Day3

    The Georgian Military Road to Kazbegi

    We set off in the morning to the north, passing the historic town of Mtskheta and Jvari Monastery overlooking the meeting of two rivers, then stop at Ananuri Fortress on the banks of the turquoise Zhinvali Reservoir. The road keeps climbing through Gudauri to the town of Stepantsminda (Kazbegi), where we ride a 4x4 up to Gergeti Trinity Church facing Mount Kazbek. Overnight in Kazbegi or Gudauri.

    • Jvari Monastery in Mtskheta
    • Ananuri Fortress
    • Zhinvali Reservoir
    • Stepantsminda (Kazbegi)
    • Gergeti Trinity Church
  4. Day4

    Gudauri and back to Tbilisi

    A mountain morning in Gudauri with a visit to the Friendship Monument overlooking a wide valley, and a chance to ride the cable car or try paragliding (optional and weather permitting). On the way back we stop at Passanauri, where you can try river rafting in summer. We reach Tbilisi in the evening with free time.

    • Friendship Monument in Gudauri
    • Gudauri cable car
    • Passanauri
    • Tbilisi
  5. Day5

    From Tbilisi to Borjomi

    We head west to Borjomi, the town famous for its mineral waters and dense forests, on a drive of about three hours. We visit the Central Park and taste the mineral water at its spring, then take the cable car up to the plateau overlooking the town. The evening is free to relax in the calm atmosphere of the forest.

    • Borjomi Central Park
    • Mineral water spring
    • Borjomi cable car
  6. Day6

    Bakuriani, then back to Tbilisi

    A short trip to the mountain resort of Bakuriani among pine forests, where you can ride the cable car and enjoy seasonal activities: snow in winter and green pastures in summer. In the afternoon we return to Tbilisi, with time to shop at one of its large shopping centers or visit Turtle Lake.

    • Bakuriani resort
    • Bakuriani cable car
    • Turtle Lake
    • Shopping centers in Tbilisi
  7. Day7

    Departure

    Breakfast at the hotel, then free time depending on your flight time. After that we transfer you to Tbilisi International Airport for your flight back to Istanbul or your next destination, with memories of the Caucasus that last a long time.

    • Hotel
    • Tbilisi International Airport

Questions & answers

Questions about the Georgia program, 7 days

Is a Turkish residence permit enough to enter Georgia?

According to the available information, a Turkish residence permit alone does not give the right to enter without a visa, and what counts is the nationality of the passport. Some nationalities enter without a visa, and others need an e-visa. Send us your nationality on WhatsApp and we will confirm the current requirements, noting that the entry decision rests with the Georgian authorities.

How many days are enough to visit Georgia?

One week is a good length to see Tbilisi, the mountains and Borjomi without rushing. If you want the sea, add two or three nights in Batumi to make the program 9 or 10 days.

Does the program include flights from Istanbul?

No. The program is priced without international flights, and we can add them on request, choosing the most suitable date. Learn about our flight booking service.

Is Georgia suitable for families in winter?

Yes, especially for snow lovers in Gudauri and Bakuriani. However, mountain roads may sometimes close because of snowstorms, so we keep the program flexible and suggest alternatives within Tbilisi when needed.

Can I travel by road from Trabzon to Batumi?

Yes, you can cross by road through the Sarp border crossing between Turkey and Georgia, and the drive from Trabzon takes about 3 hours depending on traffic at the crossing. We arrange this option within a custom program that combines both countries.
