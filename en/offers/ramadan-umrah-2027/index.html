Ramadan Umrah 2027 from Turkey: register your interest early

Ramadan Umrah 2027 from Turkey needs early planning, especially if you want the last ten days near the Haram. Register your interest now, and we will arrange flights, hotels in Makkah and Madinah and visa file preparation, in coordination with authorized Umrah service providers.

At a glance

Umrah in Ramadan is one of the most requested trips in the Muslim world, and demand peaks in the last ten days, when hotels near the Sacred Mosque get more expensive and available rooms run out quickly. That is why we opened this page to register interest early, so we can look for flights and hotels for you before options narrow. Seats on Istanbul flights to Jeddah and Madinah also usually become scarcer as the month approaches.

Ramadan 1448 AH is expected to begin around February 8 or 9, 2027, with the last ten days starting near the end of February or the start of March, all depending on the moon sighting. Umrah can be performed with the Umrah visa through the Nusuk platform or authorized Umrah agents, or with the tourist visa for those whose nationality is eligible for it, with an Umrah permit booked in the Nusuk app in both cases.

OYA Tourism is not a licensed Umrah agent. Our role is to book your flights from Istanbul, choose hotels in Makkah and Madinah to fit your budget, prepare the visa file, and coordinate with the authorized providers on Nusuk where required. The decision to issue the visa and permits rests with the competent Saudi authorities, and we make clear from the start what we handle and what the authorized provider handles.

What the service includes

  • Searching for and booking suitable flights from Istanbul.
  • Booking hotels in Makkah and Madinah in line with the visa requirements.
  • Preparing the visa file and reviewing documents before submission.
  • Coordinating with authorized Umrah service providers on Nusuk where required.
  • Guiding you in booking the Umrah permit and the Rawdah Sharifah appointment in the Nusuk app.
  • Arranging transport between Jeddah, Makkah and Madinah according to the program.

Preparation checklist

Ramadan Umrah preparation timeline

  • Now (November and December 2026): register your interest, and decide the number of travelers and their nationalities, whether you want the last ten days or the start of Ramadan, and whether to start in Makkah or Madinah.
  • As soon as possible: check that passports are valid for at least six months, and we determine the right visa route for each traveler's nationality.
  • December 2026 and January 2027: confirming flights and hotels in Makkah and Madinah, subject to availability.
  • Less than 30 days before travel: submitting the visa application, because the Umrah visa is cancelled if you do not enter the Kingdom within 30 days of its issuance.
  • Before arrival: booking the Umrah permit and the Rawdah Sharifah visit appointment in the Nusuk app, according to the available slots.
  • As a reminder: according to the 1448 AH Umrah season calendar announced by the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah, the last day for issuing visas is 1 Shawwal 1448 AH (March 9, 2027), and the last day for entry is 15 Shawwal 1448 AH (March 23, 2027).

Questions & answers

FAQ

When is Ramadan 2027?

Ramadan 1448 AH is expected to begin around February 8 or 9, 2027, and Eid al-Fitr around March 9 or 10, 2027. Final dates are set by the moon sighting.

Can Umrah be performed on the Saudi tourist visa?

Yes, for holders of nationalities eligible for the tourist visa, including Turkey, outside the Hajj season, provided you book an Umrah permit in the Nusuk app. Tourist visa eligibility follows the nationality of the passport, not the country of residence, so we check your situation before any booking.

Is OYA Tourism an authorized Umrah company?

No. We are a travel agency in Istanbul, not a licensed Umrah agent. We arrange flights, hotels and visa file preparation, and we coordinate with the authorized Umrah service providers on Nusuk where required.

When should I book a Makkah hotel for the last ten days of Ramadan?

The earlier the better, because demand for hotels near the Haram in the last ten days is high. We recommend registering your interest now and confirming the hotel as soon as your budget and dates allow.

Do I need a Nusuk permit to perform Umrah in Ramadan?

Yes. Performing Umrah requires a permit booked in the Nusuk app, whatever the type of visa, and the same applies to visiting the Rawdah Sharifah in the Prophet's Mosque. We guide you through booking both according to the available slots.

Can Syrians or Iraqis living in Turkey perform Ramadan Umrah?

They often need the dedicated Umrah visa through Nusuk or an authorized agent, because a Turkish residence permit alone does not make them eligible for the tourist visa. Those holding a used US, UK or Schengen visa may be eligible for it under the announced conditions, and issuing rules for some nationalities may differ, so we check before any payment.
