Best time to visit Turkey: a month-by-month guide for every city and season

Turkey has varied climates, so what suits Istanbul may not suit Antalya or Uzungöl in the same month. A month-by-month guide helps you choose the timing and the destination together.

Travelers often ask us: what is the best time to visit Turkey? The honest answer is that Turkey is a large country with many climates, so what suits Istanbul may not suit Antalya or Uzungöl in the same month. That is why choosing the month alone is not enough; you need to choose the month and the destination together. In this guide we go through the months one by one and suggest the best destinations for each season, along with the holiday dates that affect prices and crowds.

Please note that the weather descriptions here are approximate and vary from year to year; it is always best to check the forecast a few days before you travel.

Turkey's climate in brief: four main regions

  • Istanbul and the Marmara region (Bursa and Sapanca): warm, humid summers and cold, rainy winters with occasional snow in some years, and much more snow on Mount Uludağ.
  • The southern and western coast (Antalya and Bodrum): very hot summers and relatively mild winters, with a swimming season that runs roughly from May to October.
  • The Black Sea (Trabzon, Uzungöl and Ayder): rain in most seasons of the year and year-round greenery, pleasant mild summers, and snow in the highlands in winter.
  • Central Anatolia (Cappadocia): hot, dry summers with sunny days and milder nights, and very cold winters covered in snow.

Best time to visit Istanbul

The best times in Istanbul are spring (April and May) and autumn (September and October): pleasant weather for walking, clear skies over the Bosphorus, and fewer crowds than in summer. In April the city's gardens, especially Emirgan Park, are decked with millions of tulips.

Summer in Istanbul is warm, humid and crowded, but it still works for those whose school holidays force this timing, especially with day trips to Sapanca and the Princes' Islands to escape the heat. Winter is cold and rainy, but excellent for museums, mosques and covered markets, and prices are usually calmer.

Trabzon and Uzungöl: when to go to Turkey's Black Sea region

The golden season for Turkey's Black Sea region is May to September, when nature is at its greenest and the weather is mild. July and August are the peak of the Gulf season, so prices rise and villas with a view sell out quickly. May, June and September combine lovely weather with fewer crowds.

Remember that rain is part of the Black Sea's nature in any month, so bring a light coat. In winter Uzungöl turns into an enchanting white canvas, but some mountain roads may be affected. Read more in the Tourism in Uzungöl guide.

Antalya and Bodrum: the sea season

The swimming season in the south runs roughly from May to October. July and August are very hot and suit those who spend their day between the pool and the sea at the resorts. June and September are best for those who want to combine the sea with tours, as the sea is still warm in September while the heat eases relatively. In winter, Antalya remains a mild choice to escape the cold of other cities, but it is not swimming season. Details in the Tourism in Antalya guide.

Cappadocia and the balloon ride

Spring and autumn are best for Cappadocia, with mild weather that suits walking in the valleys, and often calm mornings for balloon flights. Summer is hot by day but pleasant in the mornings and evenings. Winter is very cold, but the sight of balloons over snow-covered valleys is among the most beautiful you can see.

Important note: balloon flights depend on wind and weather conditions and may be canceled on the same day in any season. We therefore recommend staying at least two nights. Read more about Cappadocia.

Turkey in winter: snow and skiing

For snow lovers, the season usually runs from December to March depending on the amount of snow each year. The main options:

  • Mount Uludağ in Bursa: the best-known ski destination near Istanbul, with mountain hotels and a cable car.
  • Uzungöl: a lake and snow-covered cabins in complete calm.
  • Cappadocia: rock valleys under snow and the sight of balloons.
  • Kartepe and Sapanca: winter atmosphere close to Istanbul.

Seasons and holidays: when do prices rise?

  1. The Gulf summer holidays run from about late June to August, the highest-demand season for Turkey's Black Sea region, Sapanca and the resorts.
  2. Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha: bookings rise weeks before them, so book early.
  3. New Year: Istanbul and the snow destinations get crowded.
  4. Usually the quietest periods: late autumn and winter outside the holidays, and early spring.

Traveling to Turkey in Ramadan

Ramadan 1448 is expected to begin around February 8, 2027 and to last until roughly early March. Istanbul in Ramadan has a special spiritual atmosphere: the courtyards of the great mosques fill with families just before sunset, mosques are decorated with lights, and restaurants offer special iftar menus. Restaurants and cafés usually stay open during the day, so travelers do not find it hard to get around or to eat.

If you are planning to travel in Ramadan, choose a lighter program during the day and book your iftar restaurant early in busy areas. As Eid al-Fitr approaches, demand for flights and hotels clearly rises, so do not put off booking.

Questions & answers

Related questions

What is the best month to travel to Turkey?

There is no single perfect month for all of Turkey. April, May, September and October are excellent for Istanbul and Cappadocia, June to August suits Turkey's Black Sea region and the coast, and December to March is for snow in Uludağ and Uzungöl.

Is Turkey a good destination in winter?

Yes. Winter is the season of snow and skiing in Uludağ, Uzungöl and Cappadocia, Istanbul is great for museums and markets, and prices are often calmer outside the holidays.

When is the best time to visit Trabzon and Uzungöl?

May to September is the best period, with July and August the peak of the season. Rain is possible in any month because it is part of the Black Sea climate.

When are travel prices to Turkey cheapest?

Usually outside the summer holidays, Eid and New Year, such as late autumn, winter outside the holidays and early spring, varying by destination.
