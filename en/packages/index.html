Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about programs and packages

What does the tour package include?

It varies by program and usually includes airport pickup and drop-off, hotel accommodation with breakfast, and private-car tours with an English-speaking driver. Each program page has a clear “Included / Not included” list.

Does the package include international flights?

Not by default. They can be added to the same offer, from your city or from Istanbul.

Can the program be customized to my wishes?

Yes, completely: the number of days, the order of the cities, the hotel category, the type of car, and the addition of private activities.

Is the price per person or per family?

We send you a detailed price quote based on the number of travelers, rooms and the type of car, and it states whether the price is per person or for the whole group.

What is the cancellation policy?

It depends on the hotels, the services booked and the season. We send it to you in writing with the offer, before any payment, so you are fully informed.

When should I book for the summer season and holidays?

We recommend booking several weeks in advance, especially villas and chalets in Sapanca, Trabzon and Uzungöl, because demand for them rises sharply in summer and during holidays.
