Hotels in Turkey's most beautiful cities

Istanbul Hotels

Istanbul
  • Taksim, Şişli and Nişantaşı
  • Sultanahmet and Fatih
  • The Bosphorus (Beşiktaş and Ortaköy)

In Istanbul you choose the area first and then the hotel, because the city is vast and the traffic is real. Here we summarize the areas to stay in, the types of accommodation, and what to check before booking.

Trabzon Hotels

Trabzon
  • City center and Meydan

Trabzon is the base for trips in Turkey's Black Sea region, and from it you set out to Uzungöl, Ayder and the green plateaus. Where you stay there determines how many hours you spend on the road each day.

Uzungöl Hotels

Uzungöl

Uzungöl is a small mountain village around a calm lake, and staying a night or two gives you its best hours: early morning and the evening after the day visitors leave.

Sapanca Hotels

Sapanca
  • Around the lake and Maşukiye

Sapanca is the nature destination closest to Istanbul, and its best-known feature is private bungalows with a pool or jacuzzi among the trees. A night or two here is enough for a quiet break within your city program.

Antalya Hotels

Antalya
  • Lara, Belek, Kemer and Alanya

Antalya is the resort capital of the Turkish coast, and the all-inclusive system is the most widespread there. What matters is choosing the right area and understanding what the system actually includes before you book.

Conservative hotels

Turkey

If your priority is a family atmosphere without alcohol, a separate pool for women, or a villa with a private pool away from view, these options exist in Turkey but need careful research. Here we explain where to find them and how to verify them before booking.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about hotel booking

What is the best area to stay in Istanbul for families?

Taksim and Şişli are among the most popular areas for their proximity to shopping, restaurants and transport; Sultanahmet suits those focused on historic sights, while Bosphorus hotels give you a quiet atmosphere and special views. We recommend the best fit for your plan.

Are there conservative or family-friendly hotels in Turkey?

Yes, there are hotels and resorts that respect family privacy, some with separate pools or villas with private pools, especially in Antalya, Sapanca and Trabzon. Tell us your requirements and we will choose what suits them.

Are your prices cheaper than booking sites?

We compare options honestly; in many cases we offer competitive prices or better value when the hotel is combined with transfers and a program, and sometimes the price is similar, but with OYA you get personal follow-up in English and help solving any issue with the hotel.

What is the difference between a hotel and an aparthotel?

A hotel provides full daily services such as cleaning, breakfast and reception, while an aparthotel offers more space, a kitchen and several rooms with lighter services — ideal for large families and long stays.

Do you offer villas with a private pool in Trabzon and Sapanca?

Yes, we offer villas and bungalows with private pools in Sapanca, Maşukiye, Trabzon and Bursa, and early booking is recommended in summer and holidays because demand is high.

Do you book hotels outside Turkey?

Yes, we book hotels in Mecca, Medina, Dubai, Georgia, Azerbaijan, Malaysia and elsewhere, and link them with flight tickets and visas when needed.
