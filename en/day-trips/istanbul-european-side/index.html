Istanbul European side tour: 10 hours with a private car and driver

Istanbul's most famous landmarks in one day: Sultanahmet, Hagia Sophia and the Grand Bazaar, then Galata, Taksim and the Bosphorus shore, with a private car waiting for you at every stop.

At a glance

The European side holds most of Istanbul's historic landmarks. A private car saves you the trouble of getting between districts, but the Sultanahmet area itself is visited on foot, as the sights there are close together.

Ten hours is not enough to go inside everything, so we set the priorities with you: which museums to enter, where photos are enough, and when to stop for prayer and lunch.

What the service includes

  • A private car for you and your companions only, with fuel and road and bridge tolls.
  • A driver who speaks Arabic or English; other languages on advance request, subject to availability.
  • Pickup from and return to your hotel in Istanbul.
  • A flexible program: you choose the stops and how long to stay at each.
  • Child seats on advance request.
  • Contact with our team in English via WhatsApp throughout the day.

Tour stops

What do you visit on this day?

Sultanahmet

Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, Topkapı Palace and the Basilica Cistern, all within walking distance of each other.

The Grand Bazaar and the Spice Bazaar

The oldest of Istanbul's covered markets, for gifts, sweets and spices.

Galata and Taksim

Galata Tower, İstiklal Avenue and Taksim Square.

The Bosphorus shore

Dolmabahçe Palace from the outside or the inside, then Ortaköy and its mosque beneath the bridge.

Questions & answers

FAQ

Are 10 hours enough for the sights of Istanbul's European side?

It is enough for the main landmarks, with a visit inside one or two museums. If you want to go into every museum at a relaxed pace, you need two days.

Is the driver a tour guide?

The driver takes you around and helps you get from place to place, but is not a tour guide. If you would like historical commentary, we arrange a licensed guide on request.

Are museum tickets included?

Entrance tickets are usually not included in the price of the car, and we make this clear in the quote. We can add them for you on request.
