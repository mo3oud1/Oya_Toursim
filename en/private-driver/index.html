English-speaking private driver in Istanbul, Trabzon, Bursa and Antalya

At a glance

Many families prefer a private driver to group tours, because the plan follows their own rhythm: time for prayer, a break for the children, and a stop at a restaurant you liked on the way. The driver knows the roads and where to park, helps you communicate with restaurants and shops, and suggests an order for the stops to avoid traffic. This is especially useful with older people and children, because it reduces long walks and waiting at public transport stops.

A full day usually includes the car, the driver and fuel for about 10 hours within the city or on the agreed route, and your quote states the number of hours, the distance limit and exactly what the price includes. You can book the driver by the hour for a shopping trip or dinner, or for all the days of your trip so he is with you from airport pickup to the drop-off on departure day. We agree in advance on what is not included in the price, such as extra hours and road and parking fees, so you know the cost of your day before it begins.

We arrange a driver in Istanbul, in Trabzon for visiting Uzungöl and Ayder, in Bursa for the trip up Uludağ, and in Antalya for getting between waterfalls and beaches. If you want a ready-made plan, take a look at our day trips or our tour packages that include a private driver.

What the service includes

  • The car and driver for all the agreed hours.
  • Fuel within the route and distance set in the quote.
  • Pickup from your hotel and return to it.
  • Flexibility in arranging stops, prayer times and breaks during the day.
  • Child seats on advance request.

Options

Choose what suits you

Old Istanbul in a day

Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque and Topkapı Palace, then a nearby lunch, the Grand Bazaar and the Spice Bazaar, with pickup from your hotel and return to it.

The Bosphorus and the Asian side

Ortaköy and Bebek on the European shore, then across the bridge to Çamlıca Mosque and the Üsküdar view of the Maiden's Tower at sunset.

A shopping and family day

The big shopping malls and local markets, with a stop at children's attractions such as the aquarium or Miniatürk, and your purchases stay in the car between stops.

From Trabzon to Uzungöl

A mountain road alongside the Solaklı River with stops for photos, tea and honey, then free time around Lake Uzungöl before heading back in the evening.

Bursa and Uludağ

The Uludağ cable car up the mountain, then the Green Mosque, the covered bazaar and the ancient plane tree in İnkaya.

Antalya, waterfalls and sea

Düden Waterfalls and the old town of Kaleiçi, or a day in Kemer with the Olympos cable car to the top of Tahtalı Mountain.

How it works

Simple steps

Tell us your plan

The city, dates, number of passengers and bags, and the places you would like to visit.

We suggest the plan and the car

We send you a suggested arrangement for the days and the type of car, with a written quote setting out the hours and what is included.

Booking confirmation

Once you approve, we confirm the booking and send you the driver's name and contact number.

Set off at your own pace

The driver picks you up from the hotel at the agreed time, and you adjust the stops during the day within the agreed hours.

Extra hours: if the day goes beyond the agreed hours, each extra hour is charged as stated in the quote, so tell the driver early if you would like to extend.
Road, bridge and ferry fees: the quote states whether they are included or paid separately, especially for trips to Bursa or across the Bosphorus bridges.
Parking: the quote states who covers parking fees at tourist sites and shopping malls.
Meals: guests' meals are at their own expense, and we agree in advance on the driver's meal on long days so there are no surprises.
The driver is not a licensed tour guide and does not accompany you inside museums to explain them, and we arrange a licensed guide on request.
Entrance tickets and activities such as cable cars, boat trips and museums are not included unless stated in the quote.
Trips that require the driver to stay overnight outside his home city add the cost of his accommodation, and we explain this before confirmation.

Questions & answers

FAQ

How much does a private driver in Istanbul cost per day?

The price depends on the type of car, the number of hours, the route and the season, and trips outside the city differ from tours within it. Send us your dates and number of passengers on WhatsApp and we will send you a clear written quote.

How many hours a day does a private driver work?

A full day is usually about 10 hours, and the exact number is stated in your quote. If you need more time, extra hours are charged as stated in the quote.

Is the private driver a tour guide?

The driver is English-speaking and helps you get around and communicate, but he is not a licensed tour guide. If you want an explanation inside Hagia Sophia or Topkapı Palace, we arrange a licensed guide on request.

Can I go with the driver from Istanbul to Bursa or Sapanca?

Yes, and these are popular trips for visitors to Istanbul. Trips to Bursa and Sapanca count as a day outside the city, and the quote explains any road and ferry fees.

Is there an English-speaking driver in Trabzon for visiting Uzungöl?

We arrange an English-speaking driver for you in Trabzon to visit Uzungöl, Ayder and Sümela Monastery, and he can pick you up straight from Trabzon Airport.
