Destination guide • Sapanca

Sapanca and Maşukiye tourism

Tourism in Sapanca is the nearest escape to nature from Istanbul: a wide lake, dense forests, the Maşukiye waterfalls and bungalows with private pools, about two hours away.

Best time to visit: April to October, and autumn with its golden colors is one of the loveliest seasonsSuggested duration: a day trip, or one to two nightsNearest airport: Sabiha Gökçen Airport (SAW), about 100 km away

Why Sapanca?

Sapanca at a glance

Sapanca is a quiet town in Sakarya province, east of Istanbul, stretching along a wide freshwater lake surrounded by the forested Samanlı Mountains. It is about 140 km from Istanbul, roughly two hours by car in normal conditions, which has made it a favorite for day trips and weekend breaks. Its atmosphere is completely different from the bustle of the city: fresh air, greenery all around you, and restaurants and cafés overlooking the water. It is one of the most requested destinations near Istanbul among families.

Close to the lake is Maşukiye village, part of the Kartepe district in Kocaeli province, known for its small waterfalls and the fish and grill restaurants lining the streams. From there you climb to the Kartepe highlands, with forests, viewpoints and a ski resort in winter. A typical trip combines the lake, Maşukiye and Kartepe in a single day, with lunch on the bank of a stream among the trees. On the way you stop at lake viewpoints and shops selling rural products such as honey, jams and cheeses.

What sets Sapanca apart is its bungalows and wooden chalets with private pools or jacuzzis among the trees, away from view, a popular choice for families and honeymooners. Adventure activities such as quad bikes, ziplines and boat tours on the lake add extra fun for young people and children. The bungalows range from small ones for two people to spacious ones with several rooms, and some have heated pools that work in winter.

You can visit Sapanca on a day trip from Istanbul, or stay a night or two to enjoy the calm of the lake in the evening. We include it in the 8-day Istanbul, Sapanca and Bursa program with an English-speaking driver, and we help you choose a bungalow that fits the size of your family. In autumn the forests turn yellow and orange, and in winter snow may cover the Kartepe highlands, so every season in Sapanca has its own character.

Top sights

The best sights in Sapanca

A carefully chosen list of what is worth your time in Sapanca, with the visits smartly arranged in our programs.

Sapanca Lake

A freshwater lake about 16 km long, surrounded by green hills, restaurants and cafés, its waters taking on golden tones at sunset.

The Lakeside Promenade

Walking paths, seating areas and a shoreline stroll in town, good for an evening family walk or a bike ride.

Maşukiye Village

A mountain village among trees and streams, known for its small waterfalls, its restaurants overlooking the water and its shops selling rural products.

Trout Restaurants by the Streams

The area's best-known lunch experience: grilled trout or grilled meats at tables over or beside the water, among the greenery.

Kartepe Cable Car

It takes you up from the Maşukiye area to the Kartepe highlands, with a wide view over the lake and the forests from above.

Kartepe Ski Resort

In winter the Kartepe peaks become a ski resort close to Istanbul, good for children's first snow experience.

Ormanya Nature Park

A large nature park in Kartepe with walking trails among the trees and areas for getting to know the animals, good for a quiet family outing.

Adventure Activities

Quad bikes (ATV) on forest tracks, ziplines, horse riding and boat tours on the lake. They are usually booked on site for a separate fee.

Lake Poyrazlar

A small forest-ringed lake near Adapazarı, about half an hour away, an ideal place for a stroll away from the crowds.

Sapanca Bungalows with a Private Pool

Wooden chalets among the trees with private pools or jacuzzis that offer complete privacy for families and couples, and a main reason for Sapanca's fame.

Day Trips

Tours and day trips from Sapanca

  • Sapanca and Maşukiye trip from Istanbul: a morning start, then the lakeside promenade, the Maşukiye waterfalls and lunch by the river, and the Kartepe cable car, with optional activities. You are back in the evening after a trip of about 10 hours.
  • Kartepe in winter: a day in the snow on the Kartepe highlands, with skiing or sledding for children, ending with a warm lunch in Maşukiye.
  • Lake Poyrazlar and Adapazarı: a forest walk around the small lake, then shopping in the modern malls of Sakarya province.
  • Continuing to Bursa: Sapanca can be linked with Bursa in one program without returning to Istanbul, which is what we do in the 8-day programs.

Where to stay?

Best areas to stay in Sapanca

  • Private bungalows and chalets: the most requested option: privacy, a pool or jacuzzi and a garden, suitable for families and honeymooners. Ask about the number of rooms and whether the pool is heated or covered.
  • Lakeside hotels: hotels and resorts with a direct view of the lake and full services, suitable for older travelers and for anyone who prefers hotel service.
  • Maşukiye and Kartepe: a mountain atmosphere among forests and streams, close to restaurants and the cable car, and ideal in winter for snow lovers.
  • Central Sapanca: close to the promenade, restaurants and shops, a practical and economical choice for a short stay.

OYA tips

Tips before traveling to Sapanca

  • Avoid leaving Istanbul at peak hours and on weekend mornings, as the highway can get congested and add an hour or more.
  • When booking a bungalow, check the details: the number of rooms, the type of pool (open, or covered and heated), and the distance from the lake and restaurants.
  • Activities such as quad bikes and ziplines are usually paid separately on site, so ask the price before you start.
  • Food is halal in local restaurants, and the best things to try are trout and the Turkish village breakfast with its fresh produce.
  • The lake, Maşukiye and Kartepe are fairly far apart and public transport to them is limited, so a private car with an English-speaking driver is more comfortable, especially on snowy roads in winter.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about Sapanca tourism

How far is Sapanca from Istanbul?

About 140 km east of Istanbul, roughly two hours by car in normal conditions. The trip can take longer at peak hours and on weekends.

Is a day trip enough, or is it better to stay overnight in Sapanca?

A day trip is enough for the lake and Maşukiye, but a night in a bungalow with a private pool gives you a calm evening and morning by the lake, a very different experience from a single day visit.

What is the difference between Sapanca and Maşukiye?

Sapanca is the town on the lakeshore in Sakarya province, while Maşukiye is a nearby mountain village that belongs to Kartepe in Kocaeli province and is known for its waterfalls and fish restaurants. A single trip usually combines the two.

What are the best bungalows with a private pool in Sapanca?

The best depends on the size of your family, your budget and your dates. There are small bungalows for couples and multi-room ones for families, and some have a heated pool that works in winter. Send us your dates and group size on WhatsApp and we will suggest options that are actually available.

Is Sapanca worth visiting in winter?

Yes, especially if you want snow near Istanbul. The Kartepe highlands become a ski resort, and bungalows with a jacuzzi or heated pool make the stay warm and enjoyable.

Is Sapanca suitable for children?

Very much: wide green spaces, a safe lakeside promenade, activities such as horse riding and boats, and nature parks. Just keep a close eye on children around the private pools at the bungalows.
