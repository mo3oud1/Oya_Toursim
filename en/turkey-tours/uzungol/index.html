Destination guide • Uzungöl

Tourism in Uzungöl: the lake, cabins and plateaus

Tourism in Uzungöl (also spelled Uzungol or Uzunggol) is the green dream of many visitors: a quiet lake among forested mountains, wooden cabins, and fresh air even in midsummer.

Best time to visit: May to September for greenery, December to February for snowSuggested duration: one to two nights, or a day trip from TrabzonNearest airport: Trabzon Airport (TZX), about 100 km away (around two hours by car)

Why Uzungöl?

Uzungöl at a glance

Uzungöl is a small mountain town in the Çaykara district of Trabzon province, at an altitude of just over 1,000 meters, about 100 km from Trabzon city. Its name in Turkish means "Long Lake," and the lake formed when rockslides blocked the course of the mountain river. It is now the best-known image of Turkey's Black Sea region. Visitors reach it by the coastal road to the town of Of, then a mountain road that climbs alongside the river between forests and wooden villages.

Around the lake there is an easy walking path for families, a mosque on the shore that has become the symbol of the place, and restaurants serving trout from the nearby streams. From the town, roads climb to higher plateaus such as Sultan Murat Plateau and the Demirkapı viewpoint, with open pastures and views above the clouds. Hotels and cabins look out over the water from the slopes, so the town looks like a postcard in photos.

Uzungöl is crowded in summer, receiving millions of visitors a year according to the Turkish press, many of them from the Gulf countries, and the evening by the lake with its lights becomes part of the fun. In winter it is covered in snow and turns into a quiet white scene for anyone looking for a different experience. In late spring and early autumn the lake is calmer and the forest colors are at their best, an ideal time for couples and photography lovers.

You can visit Uzungöl on a day trip from Trabzon, but we recommend staying one or two nights to enjoy the calm of early morning before the day-trippers arrive. We usually include it in the 7-day Trabzon and Uzungöl itinerary with an English-speaking driver and a hotel or cabin with a lake view, or in the 10-day Black Sea tour, which adds Ayder, Rize and Sümela Monastery. We plan your day so that you reach the lake in the morning before the crowds, with time for the nearby plateaus and for walking around the water without hurrying.

Top sights

The best places to visit in Uzungöl

A carefully chosen list of what is worth your time in Uzungöl, with smart visit planning inside our itineraries.

Lake Uzungöl and the walk around it

A level path around the lake that takes under an hour to walk, with small wooden bridges and benches for resting. It is at its best early in the morning, when the mist is reflected on the water.

Uzungöl Mosque

The mosque on the lake shore is the most famous view here. Pray in it, then take photos from the opposite bank, where its reflection falls on the water.

Sultan Murat Plateau

A wide plateau of open pastures and clean air about 35 km above Uzungöl. The road up is steep, and a driver used to mountain roads is preferable.

Demirkapı viewpoint

A road climbing above the town gives you a full view of the lake and valley from above, and leads to summer pastures and traditional shepherds' huts.

Ortaköy waterfall

A beautiful waterfall on the road between the coast and Uzungöl, where most visitors stop to take photos and drink tea.

Trout restaurants

The fish are raised in cold stream water and served grilled or fried in restaurants on the river and lake banks, with corn bread and country dishes.

Tea, honey and knife shops

On the road to Uzungöl, tours stop at tea factories, mountain honey shops and shops selling handmade Sürmene knives, which make distinctive gifts from the north.

Horse riding and mountain activities

Seasonal activities around the town include horse riding, ziplining and walking on mountain trails, and they vary with the weather and the season.

Cabins with views

Wooden houses and cabins stepped up the slopes with balconies overlooking the lake, an essential part of the overnight experience here.

Uzungöl in winter

With winter, snow covers the rooftops and forests, and the lake becomes a quiet white canvas. The roads then call for a driver used to driving in snow.

Day Trips

Tours and day trips from Uzungöl

  • Sultan Murat Plateau: Half a day on the high plateau for walks among the pastures and photos above the clouds, returning to the lake before sunset.
  • Ayder and Rize: A transfer to Ayder Plateau (about 160 km) via the tea gardens in Rize, with the option of an overnight stay there in a wooden cabin.
  • Trabzon city and Lake Sera: Back to the coast to visit Boztepe Hill and Hagia Sophia and shop in Meydan, with a stroll at Lake Sera.
  • Sümela Monastery and Zigana: A day trip to the monastery hanging on the cliff and the Zigana mountain road, usually starting from Trabzon.

Where to stay?

The best areas to stay in Uzungöl

  • Lakeside hotels: A few steps from the walking path, the restaurants and the mosque, suitable for older travelers and families with young children. Evening activity is to be expected on summer nights.
  • Cabins and bungalows on the slopes: Panoramic views and more privacy, suited to couples and honeymooners. Some are on very steep uphill roads, so a car is essential.
  • Villas and country houses in the nearby villages: More space for large families, often with private gardens, and total quiet away from the crowds.
  • Staying in Trabzon and visiting Uzungöl for a day: A practical choice for those who prefer city services or are visiting out of season, with a day trip by private car.

OYA tips

Tips before traveling to Uzungöl

  • Go early: the first hours of the morning are the quietest and best for photos, before the day-trip groups arrive.
  • Even in summer the air cools in the evening, so bring a warm jacket, and in winter, shoes suited to snow.
  • Book your stay well ahead in July and August, because hotels and cabins with good views fill up first.
  • The road from the coast to Uzungöl is mountainous and winding; a driver used to these roads, ideally an English-speaking one, makes the trip more comfortable and safer.
  • Food at local restaurants is halal, and most serve grills, fish and country dishes. There are small grocery shops for those staying in a cabin.
  • Carry some cash in Turkish lira for kiosks and small activities, as not every shop accepts cards.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about visiting Uzungöl

Where is Uzungöl and how far is it from Trabzon?

It is in the Çaykara district of Trabzon province in northeastern Turkey, about 100 km from Trabzon city and its airport, roughly two hours by car depending on stops.

Should I stay overnight in Uzungöl or just make a day trip?

A day trip is enough to see the lake, but staying one or two nights gives you the quiet morning, the lovely evening and a chance to visit the nearby plateaus without rushing. We recommend an overnight stay for nature lovers and anyone who wants some peace.

What is the best time to visit Uzungöl?

From May to September for greenery and mild weather, with July and August the busiest. September and early October give you autumn colors and fewer crowds.

Does it snow in Uzungöl in winter?

Yes, snow is normal in the winter months, especially from December to February, and amounts vary from year to year. The town is usually open, but we check road conditions before every trip.

What are the best hotels in Uzungöl for families?

For families with young children or older travelers, we recommend a hotel close to the lake for easy walking, and for large families a villa or a spacious multi-room cabin. Tell us how many of you there are and your budget, and we will suggest options that are actually available on your dates.

How do I avoid the crowds in Uzungöl?

In peak summer the lake is crowded during the day. Visit early in the morning or after the afternoon, and choose to stay overnight so you can enjoy it after the day-trippers leave.
