Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about trips outside Turkey

Do the programs include flights from Istanbul?

Program prices are usually calculated without international flights, so you are free to choose. We can add flights from Istanbul or from your city in the same offer.

Do you help with the destination visa?

Yes. We explain the entry requirements for your nationality and country of residence, and we help with e-Visas or embassy applications when needed. The decision to grant a visa rests with the official authorities.

What is the closest destination to Istanbul?

Georgia, Azerbaijan and Bosnia are among the closest and most popular destinations. The flight takes roughly two to three hours, and they offer beautiful nature and reasonable prices.

What is the best honeymoon destination from Turkey?

The Maldives and Bali for tropical romance, Thailand and Malaysia for a mix of cities and islands, and Georgia for lovers of mountains and nature. We will suggest the best fit for your season and budget.

Can I travel by land from Trabzon to Batumi?

Yes. Batumi in Georgia is close to the Turkish border, east of Trabzon, and the trip can be arranged by road as part of a program combining Turkey's Black Sea region and Georgia, provided you meet Georgia's entry requirements for your nationality.
