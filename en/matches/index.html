Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about match tickets

How do I attend a match through OYA?

Choose the match, category and number of fans in the form, or send them to us on WhatsApp, and we will send you the package offer in writing. Once you approve, we arrange the hotel and transfers and help you buy the official ticket in your name from the official channel, then send you the collection details and entry instructions.

Are the tickets electronic or paper?

It depends on the issuer of each match; most major matches use digital tickets on your phone. We explain the collection method in detail before you pay.

Do I need ID to enter the stadium?

At many matches the ticket is issued in the holder's name and a passport or ID is checked at entry, so we ask for names as they appear in your passport when you book.

Can I book adjacent seats for a family or group?

Yes, we do our best to get adjacent seats, and the earlier you book, the better your chances of adjacent seats and preferred categories.

Do you offer packages that include a hotel and flights?

Yes, you can choose the fan package (hotel + transfers + help with the official ticket) or the VIP package, and you can add flights and a sightseeing program in Turkey. If you already have your ticket, we arrange only the hotel and transfers for you.

Are you the official ticket seller?

No. OYA Tourism is an independent travel agency and is not affiliated with clubs or federations. We do not sell tickets and hold no stock of them: the ticket is bought from the official channel in your name at its official price. Our role is accommodation, transfers and helping you with the purchase process, for a service fee we explain to you in advance.
