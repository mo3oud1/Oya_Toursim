Unified GCC visa: latest updates and launch date

The unified GCC visa (known in the media as the “Gulf Schengen”) had not been officially launched as of the date this page was updated in November 2026. Here we gather the latest official announcements, and you can register your interest on WhatsApp so we can notify you as soon as it launches.

Overview

The unified GCC tourist visa is a project the GCC countries approved in principle in 2023. The idea is to let a tourist visit the six GCC countries with a single visa. Saudi officials had earlier spoken of targeting 2026 for its launch.

On September 6, 2026, the GCC Secretary-General Jasem Albudaiwi stated in Riyadh that the visa would be launched “soon”, without announcing a date, fees, procedures or period of validity. We have found no official announcement of the launch as of November 2026.

Some websites circulate news about a pilot phase or specific prices, but these do not come from an official body. That is why we do not publish any fees or durations before the official announcement, and we do not accept any application or payment for this visa before it is launched.

Until it launches, you can travel with a separate visa for each country, and we help you coordinate an itinerary that combines more than one Gulf country through the Gulf visas page. Register your interest on WhatsApp, and we will contact you as soon as the official announcement of the conditions and procedures is issued.

Who can apply?

  • Tourists who want to visit more than one Gulf country on a single trip.
  • Residents of Turkey of various nationalities who are planning a Gulf tour.
  • Eligibility conditions have not been officially announced yet, and we will explain them here as soon as they are issued.

Questions & answers

Unified GCC visa FAQ

When does the unified GCC visa start?

No official date had been announced as of November 2026. The last official statement was on September 6, 2026, when the GCC Secretary-General said the launch would be “soon”. Register your interest on WhatsApp and we will notify you as soon as it is announced.

How much will the unified GCC visa cost?

No fees have been officially announced, and any figures currently circulating are unofficial. We will publish the fees as soon as the relevant authorities announce them.

Can I apply for it now through OYA Tourism?

No, because it has not been launched yet. You can register your interest now, and in the meantime we help you get a visa for each country separately for your upcoming trip.

Which countries will the unified GCC visa cover?

The idea behind the project is to visit the six GCC countries: Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain and Kuwait. The operational details have not been announced yet.

What is the alternative if I want to visit more than one Gulf country now?

You get a visa for each country according to your nationality, and we arrange a complete itinerary with flights and hotels. Start from the Gulf visas page.
