Egypt Tour Program, 8 Days: Cairo, the Pyramids and Sharm El Sheikh

Three nights among the Giza pyramids and the treasures of Cairo, then four nights on the beaches of Sharm El Sheikh and the Red Sea reefs.

Cairo → Giza → Sharm El SheikhFamilyCitiesBeaches
Book this programThe program day by day

About the program

Egypt tours are a journey between a civilization thousands of years old and one of the most beautiful seas in the world. The 8-day Egypt program combines Cairo and Giza, with the Pyramids, the Sphinx, the Grand Egyptian Museum and Islamic Cairo, and Sharm El Sheikh, with its coral reefs and Red Sea resorts. Egypt is a welcoming destination with a cheerful spirit, and it surprises you with something new on every visit, from the Grand Egyptian Museum to the beaches of Sinai.

The flight from Istanbul to Cairo is short, about two hours, then we take a domestic flight to Sharm El Sheikh in about an hour. The program suits families and first-time visitors to Egypt: cultural tours at the start with a guide who explains the history, then days of relaxing, swimming and diving at the end. Sharm El Sheikh can be replaced with Hurghada on request. We schedule tours early in the morning whenever possible to avoid the heat and the crowds at the famous sites.

Trip highlights

  • The Giza pyramids and the Sphinx
  • The Grand Egyptian Museum and the treasures of King Tutankhamun
  • The Citadel of Saladin and the Mosque of Muhammad Ali
  • Al-Azhar Mosque, Al-Muizz Street and Khan El Khalili
  • Dinner on a boat on the Cairo Nile
  • Ras Mohammed Reserve and snorkeling in the Red Sea
  • Naama Bay and the Old Market in Sharm El Sheikh

The program day by day

  1. Day1

    Arrival in Cairo

    Meet and greet at Cairo International Airport, then transfer to the hotel. In the evening, enjoy dinner on a Nile boat with views of Cairo's lights from the river.

    • Cairo International Airport
    • Nile Corniche
    • Nile dinner
  2. Day2

    The Pyramids and the Grand Egyptian Museum

    In the morning we visit the Giza Plateau: the Great Pyramid (Khufu), the pyramids of Khafre and Menkaure, and the Sphinx, with a stop at the panoramic viewpoint over the three pyramids. In the afternoon we visit the Grand Egyptian Museum, close to the plateau, home to the treasures of King Tutankhamun and the colossal statue of Ramses II in the grand hall.

    • Pyramid of Khufu
    • Pyramids of Khafre and Menkaure
    • The Sphinx
    • The Grand Egyptian Museum
  3. Day3

    Islamic Cairo and Khan El Khalili

    We climb up to the Citadel of Saladin and visit the Mosque of Muhammad Ali, whose domes overlook Cairo, then go down to Al-Azhar Mosque and Al-Muizz Street with its Mamluk mosques and buildings. We finish at Khan El Khalili to shop and sit in one of its historic local cafés.

    • Citadel of Saladin
    • Mosque of Muhammad Ali
    • Al-Azhar Mosque
    • Al-Muizz Street
    • Khan El Khalili
  4. Day4

    To Sharm El Sheikh

    A short domestic flight to Sharm El Sheikh, then check-in at the resort. The rest of the day is for swimming and relaxing, and in the evening a stroll around Naama Bay with its restaurants and seaside promenade.

    • Sharm El Sheikh International Airport
    • Resort
    • Naama Bay
  5. Day5

    Ras Mohammed Reserve

    A boat trip to Ras Mohammed National Park, one of the best-known dive sites in the Red Sea, with snorkeling stops among coral reefs and colorful fish, and lunch on board. Return to the resort in the afternoon.

    • Sharm El Sheikh marina
    • Ras Mohammed Reserve
    • Coral reefs
  6. Day6

    Free day at the resort and the Old Market

    A day to relax on the beach and by the resort pools. In the evening we visit the Old Market in Sharm El Sheikh and Al Sahaba Mosque with its striking architecture, with time to buy souvenirs and spices.

    • Resort beach
    • The Old Market
    • Al Sahaba Mosque
  7. Day7

    Desert safari or Dahab (optional)

    Choose between a quad-bike safari in the Sinai desert with a Bedouin dinner and stargazing, or a day trip to Dahab with its calm atmosphere and the famous Blue Hole. You can also stay at the resort if you prefer to rest.

    • Sinai desert safari
    • Bedouin dinner
    • Dahab and the Blue Hole (optional)
  8. Day8

    Departure

    Breakfast at the resort, then transfer to Sharm El Sheikh Airport for your flight back to Istanbul, direct or via Cairo depending on the flight schedule.

    • Resort
    • Sharm El Sheikh International Airport

Questions & answers

Questions about the Egypt 8-day program

What is the best time to visit Sharm El Sheikh?

Spring and autumn are the most pleasant for combining swimming and sightseeing, and winter is warm during the day, suitable if you want to escape the cold. Summer is very hot, but suits those who spend their time between the sea and the pool.

Do I need a visa for Egypt if I live in Turkey?

What counts is the nationality of your passport, not where you live. Some nationalities get the visa easily, while others need prior approval that takes time. Send us your nationality and we will confirm the current requirements; the final decision rests with the Egyptian authorities.

Are the entrance fees for the Pyramids and the Grand Egyptian Museum included?

They are not included by default because they change from time to time, but they can be added to your offer. We will give you the published prices at the time of booking.

Sharm El Sheikh or Hurghada?

Sharm El Sheikh is known for Ras Mohammed Reserve and its proximity to Dahab and the Sinai desert, while Hurghada is a larger city with a wide choice of family resorts. Both are lovely options, and we adjust the program to your wishes.
