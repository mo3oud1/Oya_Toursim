Eid al-Fitr 2027 offers in Turkey: Istanbul, the Black Sea region and Antalya

Eid al-Fitr 2027 offers in Turkey start with early planning. Eid is expected around March 9 or 10, 2027, depending on the moon sighting, and it is a busy travel season for families from Turkey and abroad alike. We arrange your flights, hotel and driver in one program that fits your holiday days.

At a glance

Eid al-Fitr 2027 falls at the start of spring, a good time to visit Turkey before the heat and crowds of summer. But the Eid days themselves are peak season: many families from the Gulf and other countries travel in the same week, and Eid al-Fitr (Ramazan Bayramı) is an official holiday in Turkey, when Turkish families also travel between cities.

That is why we recommend booking flights and hotels early, especially family rooms, suites and the bungalows in Sapanca, which are limited in number and fill up early in busy seasons. Booking early gives you a wider choice of hotels and flight times, and makes it easier to arrange the driver who stays with you throughout the trip. Those who book before Ramadan usually have more flexibility in choosing direct flights and arrival times that suit the children.

In March, Istanbul is cool to mild with rain showers, snow may remain on the summit of Uludağ in early March, and Antalya is warmer and sunnier. Choose from the suggested programs below or ask for a custom one, and we will suggest the best way to divide your holiday days. We can also combine two cities with different atmospheres, such as Istanbul with Antalya, so the family can enjoy both city and sun.

What the service includes

  • Flights to Istanbul, Trabzon or Antalya, and domestic flights between cities.
  • Hotels, resorts and serviced apartments to suit your family size.
  • Airport pickup and drop-off.
  • A private English-speaking driver for daily tours.
  • A suggested daily program that takes the Eid crowds into account.

Preparation checklist

Early booking checklist

  • Leave a day or two of flexibility in your travel date, because the date of Eid depends on the moon sighting.
  • Book flights first, as seats for Eid week may fill up early.
  • Check that all travelers' passports are valid and the entry requirements for Turkey for your nationality, including whether you need an e-Visa.
  • Send the number of adults and children and their ages so we can choose suitable rooms and child seats in the car.
  • Choose the hotel category and preferred area, such as Taksim, Sultanahmet or the Bosphorus shore.
  • Pack light winter clothes and an umbrella, and snow clothing if Uludağ or the Black Sea region is part of your program.
The Eid date is approximate and set by the moon sighting, and the day of Eid may differ between your country and Turkey.
Prices in Eid week are usually higher than in regular weeks, and availability shrinks as the date approaches. We do not publish fixed prices; we send you an offer based on your dates.
During the Eid days the roads to Bursa, Sapanca and the famous sights get crowded, so we start tours early and avoid peak hours.
Some traditional markets may close or run shorter hours on the first days of Eid, and some museums may open late, so we plan shopping and museum visits on suitable days.
Mountain trips and balloon rides depend on the weather in March, and we suggest alternatives if conditions change.

Questions & answers

FAQ

When is Eid al-Fitr 2027?

Eid al-Fitr 1448 AH is expected around March 9 or 10, 2027. The final date is set by the moon sighting in each country, so we recommend leaving a day or two of flexibility in your travel dates.

Is Turkey crowded during Eid al-Fitr?

Usually yes. Eid is an official holiday in Turkey, when Turkish families travel between cities, and it coincides with many families traveling from abroad. We ease this by starting tours early and choosing quieter times for the busiest places.

What is the weather like in Turkey in March?

In Istanbul it is cool to mild during the day with a chance of rain. In Trabzon and the Black Sea region it is colder with fog, in Cappadocia nights are very cold, while Antalya is warmer and often sunny. These are general patterns and vary from year to year.

Is there snow in Turkey in March?

Snow may remain on Uludağ near Bursa in early March, and also in the mountain highlands of the Black Sea region, but it is not guaranteed and depends on the season. We check conditions before your trip date and suggest a suitable alternative.

When should I book an Eid al-Fitr trip to Turkey?

The earlier you book, the better, ideally before the expected start of Ramadan around February 8 or 9, 2027. Booking early gives you a wider choice of flights and family rooms.

Can you customize an Eid program instead of the ready-made ones?

Yes. The suggested programs are only a starting point. We can adjust the number of days, the cities and the hotel category, or add Cappadocia or Antalya to an Istanbul trip.
