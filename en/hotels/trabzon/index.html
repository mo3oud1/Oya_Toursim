Trabzon hotels: a stay guide between sea and mountains

Trabzon is the base for trips in Turkey's Black Sea region, and from it you set out to Uzungöl, Ayder and the green plateaus. Where you stay there determines how many hours you spend on the road each day.

Trabzon hotels: what you should know

When searching for Trabzon hotels, you will find a variety of options: hotels in the city center, serviced apartments with Black Sea views, and countryside villas with private pools among the green hills. The most important question is not “which hotel?” but “where should I stay?”, because the city is a starting point for long day trips to the plateaus and lakes, and mountain roads eat up time. The experience differs greatly between a hotel in the heart of the city where you can walk to restaurants and markets, and a quiet villa among the trees where you need a car for every outing.

Families who want restaurants and shopping nearby choose the city center around Meydan Square; those who prefer quiet and the sea head to the coast at Yomra to the east near the airport, or Akçaabat to the west of the city. Large families looking for privacy lean toward the surrounding countryside villas, which usually have a garden and pool but need a car with a driver for daily travel. Many people split their stay: most nights in Trabzon, and one or two nights in Uzungöl or Ayder for the mountain experience. If your trip starts in Istanbul, the flight to Trabzon takes about two hours, and the airport is very close to the city and the coast.

Demand for hotels and villas in the area rises sharply in July and August with the Gulf holiday season, so booking early gives you a wider choice. The weather in the north changes quickly, so it helps to have comfortable accommodation to return to on rainy days, with a flexible plan you can adjust to the fog. At OYA Tourism we help you choose the area and type of stay according to your plans and the number of people in your family, and we arrange a private car with an English-speaking driver. Sea views and long stays

Villas with a garden and often a private pool in the surrounding villages and hills, with space for large families. Complete privacy and a natural setting, but far from services, and the roads to them can be narrow and steep, so a car with a driver is a must.

Types of stay

Where to stay in Trabzon

City hotels

Hotels in different categories in the center and along the coastal road, suited to those who go out on a day tour and return in the evening. Ask about a sea view and parking.

Serviced apartments with sea views

Several rooms, a living room and a kitchen on the coast, suited to families staying longer who like the freedom of cooking and family gatherings.

Villas with a private pool

Complete privacy with a garden and pool; the pool is often open-air and unheated, so ask about it if you are traveling outside summer. Some may require a minimum number of nights.

Mountain wooden cabins

In Uzungöl, Ayder and the plateaus, a warm countryside experience with stunning views, but with simpler space and facilities than city hotels.

Before you book

Check these details

  • The exact location and the driving distance to the city center and the airport
  • The view type: sea or mountain, and from the room itself or only from the balcony
  • For villas: the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, the type of pool, and whether it is private or shared
  • Air conditioning for the humid summer, and heating in the mountain cabins
  • Parking and how easily a car can reach the property, as some villas are on narrow, steep roads
  • Is breakfast included, or is the stay without meals?
  • The cancellation policy, and whether villas have a refundable deposit and the conditions for its return

OYA tips

Booking tips

  • July and August are the peak of the Gulf season and the good villas sell out first, so book well in advance. May, June and September are quieter, and the nature is beautiful then.
  • Do not change hotels every night: make Trabzon your base, and add one or two nights in Uzungöl or Ayder depending on the plan.
  • Distances are long and the roads are mountainous, so a private car with an English-speaking driver is the practical choice, especially if you stay in a countryside villa.
  • If you are traveling in spring or autumn, ask about pool heating, as an open-air unheated pool is cold outside summer.
  • If you plan to cook in a villa or apartment, ask about the nearest market or grocery store before you arrive.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about Trabzon hotels

What is the best area to stay in Trabzon for families?

The city center around the square is the easiest choice for families who want restaurants and shopping close by, while the coast at Yomra and Akçaabat is quieter with sea views. For large families looking for privacy, countryside villas with a private pool are an excellent choice, provided you have a car with a driver.

Are there villas for rent with a private pool in Trabzon?

Yes, there are countryside villas with a garden and private pool around Trabzon, and some sleep large families. Demand for them is high in summer, so we recommend booking early and checking the number of rooms, the type of pool and the deposit terms before you pay.

Should I stay in Trabzon or in Uzungöl?

Often the best option is to combine them: most nights in Trabzon for easy services and tours, and one or two nights in Uzungöl to enjoy the lake morning and evening, away from the day-trip crowds.

When do Trabzon hotel prices rise?

Prices usually rise in July and August and during holiday seasons, when Gulf tourism peaks, and are calmer in May, June and September. Winter is quieter in the city, but it is the season for snow lovers on the plateaus.

How far is Trabzon Airport from the hotels?

Trabzon Airport (TZX) is very close, about 6 km from the city center, and the coastal town of Yomra is closer still. The distance to countryside villas varies by location, so ask before booking.

Are Trabzon hotels suitable for conservative families?

Trabzon is a relatively conservative city that many visitors choose, and the food in its restaurants is usually halal. Hotel policies do vary, though, so if you have a specific request, such as no alcoholic drinks being served, tell us and we will recommend what suits you.
