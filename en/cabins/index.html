Questions & answers

Questions about cabins & bungalows

What is the difference between a chalet, a bungalow and a cabin in Sapanca?

The three names describe almost the same thing: a small detached house among the trees, usually wooden, with a private pool or jacuzzi. "Bungalow" is the common name in Turkey, while travellers often search for "chalet" or "cabin". What matters is to check the number of bedrooms, and whether the pool is private and heated.

Are the photos of the actual cabin?

Yes, we show photos of the cabin we book for you. If anything about the cabin changes before you arrive, we tell you before confirming.

Is the listed price final?

The listed price is per night and varies by season, day of the week and number of nights. We send you the final price in writing, along with availability, before any payment.

Is the pool private and heated?

It varies from cabin to cabin. In your offer we explain whether the pool or jacuzzi is private to the cabin and whether it is heated in winter.

Can you arrange pickup and a car?

Yes, we arrange airport pickup and private car transfers with a driver, plus day trips in the area.
