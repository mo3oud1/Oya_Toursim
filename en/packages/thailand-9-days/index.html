Thailand 9-day tour program: Bangkok, Phuket and Krabi

The energy of Bangkok and its markets, then the Phi Phi Islands and Phang Nga Bay from Phuket, and the beaches and four islands of Krabi.

Bangkok → Phuket → KrabiHoneymoonFriendsFamily

About the program

A Thailand tour combines a vibrant big city with some of the most beautiful tropical islands in Asia. The 9-day Thailand tour begins in Bangkok with its palaces and markets, moves on to Phuket for island trips, and ends in calmer Krabi with its beaches and limestone rocks. Thailand is famous for its vast markets, its white-sand beaches and turquoise water, and the warm hospitality visitors feel everywhere.

Direct flights from Istanbul to Bangkok are available, and we arrange a short domestic flight to Phuket and then a road transfer to Krabi. The program suits newlyweds, friends and families, and we choose hotels in quiet areas close to halal restaurants and markets, with speedboat trips to the most beautiful islands. We leave enough free time for shopping and relaxing on the beach, and the program can be adjusted by adding Pattaya or reducing the tours to suit you.

Trip highlights

  • The Grand Palace, Wat Arun and a Chao Phraya River cruise
  • The floating market at Damnoen Saduak
  • The Phi Phi Islands, Pileh Lagoon and Maya Bay
  • James Bond Island and the floating village of Koh Panyi in Phang Nga Bay
  • Krabi four islands tour and Phra Nang Cave Beach
  • Sunset from Promthep Cape in Phuket
  • Shopping at Asiatique and ICONSIAM

The program day by day

  1. Day1

    Arrive in Bangkok

    Pick-up at Suvarnabhumi International Airport and transfer to the hotel. In the evening we visit Asiatique on the banks of the Chao Phraya River, with its shops, restaurants and Ferris wheel overlooking the river.

    • Suvarnabhumi International Airport
    • Asiatique
  2. Day2

    Historic Bangkok tour

    We visit the Grand Palace with its gilded buildings and the Temple of the Emerald Buddha (Wat Phra Kaew), then cross the river by boat to Wat Arun. In the afternoon we shop at ICONSIAM on the river, or in the Siam area and its major malls.

    • Grand Palace
    • Wat Phra Kaew
    • Wat Arun
    • Chao Phraya River
    • ICONSIAM
  3. Day3

    Floating market and shopping day

    We head out in the morning to the Damnoen Saduak floating market, where vendors sell fruit and food from their wooden boats. We return to Bangkok at midday for free time in the markets, or to visit Safari World with the children (optional).

    • Damnoen Saduak Floating Market
    • Bangkok markets
    • Safari World (optional)
  4. Day4

    To Phuket

    A short domestic flight to Phuket, then check in at the hotel. In the afternoon, an island tour that includes the Karon Viewpoint over its successive beaches, then watching the sunset from Promthep Cape.

    • Phuket International Airport
    • Karon Viewpoint
    • Promthep Cape
  5. Day5

    Phi Phi Islands

    A speedboat trip to the Phi Phi Islands: Pileh Lagoon with its turquoise water, the famous Maya Bay, and Viking Cave seen from the sea, with chances to snorkel and have lunch on Phi Phi Don. We return to Phuket in the afternoon.

    • Pileh Lagoon
    • Maya Bay
    • Viking Cave
    • Phi Phi Don Island
  6. Day6

    Phang Nga Bay and James Bond Island

    A boat tour among the towering limestone rocks of Phang Nga Bay, with canoeing inside sea caves. We visit the famous James Bond Island and have lunch in the floating village of Koh Panyi, a Muslim fishing village built on stilts over the water.

    • Phang Nga Bay
    • Sea cave canoeing
    • James Bond Island
    • Koh Panyi Village
  7. Day7

    From Phuket to Krabi

    We travel by road to Krabi in about three hours and check in at a hotel near Ao Nang Beach. In the afternoon there is free time on the beach among the limestone rocks, and in the evening a stroll through the markets and restaurants of Ao Nang.

    • Road from Phuket to Krabi
    • Ao Nang Beach
    • Ao Nang markets
  8. Day8

    Krabi four islands tour

    A boat trip to the four islands: Phra Nang Cave Beach, Chicken Island, Tup Island, which is joined by a sandbar that appears at low tide, and Poda Island with its white beach. Plenty of time for swimming, snorkeling and photos.

    • Phra Nang Cave Beach
    • Chicken Island
    • Tup Island
    • Poda Island
  9. Day9

    Departure

    Transfer to Krabi Airport for a domestic flight to Bangkok, and from there your return flight to Istanbul at the scheduled time. You can also depart from Phuket Airport if it suits your flight schedule better.

    • Krabi Airport
    • Suvarnabhumi International Airport

Included in the program

  • Airport pickup and drop-off by private car
  • 8 nights with breakfast: 3 in Bangkok, 3 in Phuket and 2 in Krabi
  • Domestic flight tickets: Bangkok–Phuket, and Krabi–Bangkok on departure
  • Trips to the Phi Phi Islands, Phang Nga Bay and the four Krabi islands, with lunch
  • The road transfer from Phuket to Krabi and the tours listed
  • Follow-up and support on WhatsApp throughout the trip

Not included

  • Flights from Istanbul (can be added on request)
  • National marine park fees and entrance tickets to palaces and temples
  • Visa fees for nationalities that need one
  • Travel insurance
  • Meals not mentioned and personal expenses

Questions & answers

Questions about the Thailand 9-day tour

Thailand for families or for couples?

It suits both: families enjoy the islands, parks and markets, while couples find romantic resorts and magical sunsets. We choose quiet areas to stay, such as Kata, Karon and Ao Nang, away from the nightlife bustle.

Do I need a visa for Thailand?

It depends on your nationality: some nationalities are exempt for a short stay, while others need an e-visa applied for before travel. In all cases, the digital arrival card must be completed before arrival. Send us your nationality and we will confirm the current requirements.

Phuket or Krabi?

Phuket is bigger, with more services and boat trips, while Krabi is calmer, with its limestone scenery and beaches. That is why we combined the two in this program, so you can enjoy both.

Can I combine Thailand and Malaysia in one trip?

Yes, it is a popular choice. Take a look at the Malaysia 9-day tour and tell us how many days you have, and we will design a combined trip across the two countries.
