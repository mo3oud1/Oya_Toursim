Bali 9-day tour program: Ubud, Nusa Penida and the southern beaches

Green days among Ubud's rice terraces and forests, then the cliffs of Nusa Penida and the calm beaches of Nusa Dua.

Ubud → Kintamani → Nusa Dua → Nusa PenidaHoneymoonNatureBeaches

About the program

A Bali tour is a dream for many newlyweds and families: a green Indonesian island that brings rice terraces, volcanoes, waterfalls and beaches together in one place. The 9-day Bali tour begins with three nights in Ubud, the green heart of the island, then five nights on the south coast with a boat trip to Nusa Penida island. Bali is famous for its private villas with pools set in nature, its quiet resorts on the Indian Ocean, and the arts and crafts markets of Ubud.

We chose one coherent route within Bali instead of hopping between Indonesian islands, so you spend more time enjoying yourself and less time on the road. Flights from Istanbul to Bali International Airport are available, and their duration varies by route and airline. If you like the cool mountain atmosphere of Puncak and Bandung, you can add them before Bali in a longer program. We pace the tours comfortably to suit newlyweds and families alike.

Trip highlights

  • Tegallalang rice terraces and the Monkey Forest in Ubud
  • View of Mount Batur and its volcanic lake from Kintamani
  • Ulun Danu Beratan Temple on Lake Beratan in the highlands
  • Sunset at Tanah Lot Temple on a rock in the sea
  • Boat trip to Nusa Penida and the Kelingking Beach viewpoint
  • Water sports at Tanjung Benoa and the beaches of Nusa Dua
  • Kecak dance performance on the cliffs of Uluwatu

The program day by day

  1. Day1

    Arrive in Bali and head to Ubud

    Pick-up at Bali International Airport (Denpasar) and drive to Ubud, about an hour and a half depending on traffic. Check in at a villa or hotel among the trees, then enjoy a quiet evening to rest after the long journey.

    • Ngurah Rai International Airport
    • Ubud
  2. Day2

    Ubud tour

    We start with the Tegallalang rice terraces in the morning, when the light is at its best, and you can try the swing overlooking the valley (optional). Then we visit the Monkey Forest, Ubud Palace and the arts and crafts market. The evening is free in Ubud's cafés and restaurants.

    • Tegallalang terraces
    • Monkey Forest
    • Ubud Palace
    • Ubud Art Market
  3. Day3

    Kintamani and Mount Batur

    On the way we visit a coffee and spice plantation to taste Balinese coffee, then drive up to Kintamani for a wide view of Mount Batur and its volcanic lake, with lunch overlooking the scenery. On the way back we visit Tirta Empul Temple, known for its spring waters, then return to Ubud.

    • Coffee and spice plantation
    • Kintamani
    • Mount Batur and its lake
    • Tirta Empul Temple
  4. Day4

    The highlands and Tanah Lot, then the south coast

    We leave Ubud for the Bedugul highlands and visit Ulun Danu Beratan Temple, which seems to float on the lake, and stop at the famous Handara Gate. In the afternoon we head to Tanah Lot Temple to watch the sunset over the sea rock, then transfer to our hotel in the south.

    • Bedugul
    • Ulun Danu Beratan Temple
    • Handara Gate
    • Tanah Lot Temple
    • Nusa Dua
  5. Day5

    Nusa Penida trip

    We set off by speedboat from Sanur to Nusa Penida island, a short trip of about 45 minutes. We visit the famous Kelingking Beach viewpoint, Broken Beach with its rock arch, and the natural Angel's Billabong. We return to Bali in the afternoon.

    • Sanur Harbor
    • Kelingking Beach
    • Broken Beach
    • Angel's Billabong
  6. Day6

    Beach and water sports day

    A flexible day on the calm beaches of Nusa Dua. For adventure lovers, nearby Tanjung Benoa offers water sports such as parasailing, banana boat rides and snorkeling (optional), or you can relax with a traditional Balinese spa session.

    • Nusa Dua Beach
    • Tanjung Benoa
    • Spa center
  7. Day7

    Uluwatu and Jimbaran

    The morning is free at the resort. In the afternoon we visit Uluwatu Temple, built on the edge of a steep cliff above the Indian Ocean, and watch the traditional Kecak dance at sunset. We finish with a seafood dinner on the sand of Jimbaran Bay.

    • Uluwatu Temple
    • Kecak dance performance
    • Jimbaran Bay
  8. Day8

    Free day and shopping

    A free day to relax at the resort or shop in Seminyak and Kuta, with local boutiques and seaside cafés. On request, we can arrange a visit to Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park or another boat trip.

    • Seminyak
    • Kuta
    • Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park
  9. Day9

    Departure

    A last breakfast at the resort and free time depending on your flight, then transfer to Bali International Airport for your return flight to Istanbul.

    • Resort
    • Ngurah Rai International Airport

Included in the program

  • Airport pick-up and drop-off in Bali by private car
  • 8 nights with breakfast: 3 in Ubud and 5 in Nusa Dua or Jimbaran
  • The tours listed, by private car with driver
  • Nusa Penida speedboat trip with transport on the island

Questions & answers

Questions about the Bali 9-day tour

Bali or Puncak for a family?

Bali suits those who love the sea, tropical nature and resorts, while Puncak suits those looking for a cool mountain atmosphere and family chalets near Jakarta. You can combine them in a longer program, and we will suggest the split based on your children's ages and your interests.

Is there a tourist tax in Bali?

Yes. The Bali government charges a one-time tourist tax for each foreign visitor, set at 150,000 Indonesian rupiah as of the latest announced update, and it may change. We will explain how to pay it before you travel.

Is Bali good for a honeymoon?

Absolutely. It is one of the most popular destinations for newlyweds: private-pool villas in Ubud, magical sunsets at Tanah Lot and Uluwatu, and dinner on the sands of Jimbaran. We keep the pace of the tours gentle to suit a honeymoon.

Is halal food available in Bali?

Most people in Bali are Hindu, but halal food is available in the main tourist areas such as Nusa Dua, Kuta, Seminyak and Ubud. We recommend halal restaurants near your hotel.
